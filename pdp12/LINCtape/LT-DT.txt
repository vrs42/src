LINCtape Mark track codes:
--------------------------
0000 End Mark
1111 Interblock Mark
1110 Forward Block Mark
0111 Reverse Block Mark
0010 Guard Mark
1001 Data Mark
1011 Final Mark
0001 Check Mark

"Forward" tape motion is reversed from DECtape.

DECtape Mark track codes:
--------------------------
101101 55 Reverse End Mark
010101 25 Interblock Mark 
010110 26 Forward Block Mark
011010 32 Reverse Guard Mark
001000 10 Lock Mark
001000 10 Reverse PCC Mark
001000 10 Reverse Final Mark
001000 10 Reverse Prefinal Mark
111000 70 Data Mark
111011 73 Prefinal Mark
111011 73 Final Mark
111011 73 PCC Mark
111011 73 Reverse Lock Mark
101001 51 Guard Mark
100101 45 Reverse Block Mark
010101 25 Interblock Mark
010010 22 End Mark

Two 18 bit words encode three 12 bit words.  Blocks are typically 86 18-bit
words long, which is to say 129 12-bit words.

The symmetry of the DECtape Mark track codes allows reading it in either
direction.
