Doug's version of CLINE is replicated here as "clinek".

Versions of CLINE, PLOTR and GRAPH likely to work with focal-69 are in
"noext".  These versions over-write and disable FATN, FLOG, and FEXP,
and are not compatible with the 4WORD overlay.

The dec-08-aj3e-pb1 is the documented CLINE, which is compatible with
4WORD and other overlays.
