CIRCUITS
8-167

D. Whiteley, International Computers, Ltd., Kidsgrove,
Stoke-on-Trent, Staffs., United Kingdom

CIRCUITS is a program which enables Electronic Circuits to
be drawn using the DEC-338 Display system. The complete
circuit can be stored on paper tape and read in for future
modifications.

Minimum Hardware:
A PDP-8 with 338 Display,
Teletype, High-Speed Reader
and Punch, and character generator

Catalog:November 1969
