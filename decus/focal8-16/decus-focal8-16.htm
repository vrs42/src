One-Sample Statistics: Two-Sample Statistics: Welch
FOCAL8-16
Procedure; One-Way Analysis of Variance; Sheffe's Contrast
Between Means

M. J. McKeown, University of Chicago, Department of
Obstetrics and Gynecology, Chicago, Illinois

A three part program used to perform one-sample and two-
sample statistics, Welch Procedure; One-Way Analysis of
Variance; and Sheffe's Contrast between Means, which
allows one to investigate more thoroughly the source of the
difference between group means.

Catalog: July 1973
