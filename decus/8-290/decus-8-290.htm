Skinny BIN Loader
8-290

Garth Peterson, Institute for Atmospheric Sciences, South
Dakota School of Mines and Technology, Rapid City, South
Dakota

This is a condensed paper tape binary loader, designed to
avoid conflict with the TC01 DECtape Library bootstrap or
with the disk data break.

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8 with Teletype, High Speed
Reader is desirable
Other Programs Needed:
RIM Loader
Source Language:
PAL-D

Catalog: July 1973
