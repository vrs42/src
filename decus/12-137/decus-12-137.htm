PAL12D
12-137
Author: Larry Davis, Washington University, St. Louis, Missouri
Submitted by: Robert Hassinger, Liberty Mutual Research Center, Hopkinton, Massachusetts
PAL12D (Davis) is a modification of the PAL-8 Assembler to allow either PDP-8 or LINC mnemonics.
Minimum Hardware: PS/8, OS/8, OS/12 Configuration
Storage Requirement: 8K
Source Language: PAL-8
Catalog: July 1974
