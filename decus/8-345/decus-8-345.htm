EDIT-PAL
8-345

F. C. Owen, General Roi Iway Signal Company, Rochester,
New York

EDIT-PAL is an overlay and addition to provide a coupling
between Editor (DEC-08-ESAB) and PAL III Assembler (DEC-08-ASB1)
plus listing line numbers and reporting the vacant
buffer balance.

Minimum Hardware:
8K PDP-8, ASR33
Other Programs Needed:
Editor (DEC-08-ESAB) and
PAL III (DEC-08-ASB1)
Storage Requirement:
Field 0, 0000-1627 and buffer
Field 1, PAL III
Source Language:
PAL III

Catalog: July 1973
