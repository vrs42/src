COMPAREM
L-55
Author: I. D. Pugsley, Department of Physiology and Biophysics, University of Washington, Seattle, Washington
COMPAREM will compare the contents of two sets of LINCtape blocks with errors displayed on the LINC scope. When an error is found, options allow the two files to become "out-of-step" if required, for example, to allow comparison of source manuscripts with insertions or deletions.
Source Language: LAP6
Storage Requirement: The manuscript occupies 32(8) blocks of tape; the binary occupies 3 blocks. During execution, COMPAREM uses 8 memory quarters.
Catalog: November 1969
