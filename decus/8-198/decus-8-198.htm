SYSHLP: Monitor System Utility Program
8-198

David M. Kristol; Moore School of Electrical Engineering,
University of Pennsylvania, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania

SYSHLP is a combined version of DNHELP (DECUS No. 8-135)
and SYSLUK (DECUS No. 8-141). Besides more convenient
alternation between the two programs, SYSHLP features
improved search coding in the SYSLUK portion.

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8 with DF32 or TC01

Other Programs Needed:
System Monitor Head (DEC-D8-
SDAA)

Source Language:
PDPMAP (DECUS No. 8-166)

Storage Requirement:
2008 - 21778

Catalog: November 1969
