CALENDAR
8-388

R. Lee, University of Kent at Canterbury, Canterbury, Kent,
England

This program will print the calendar for any year between 1
A.D. and 9999 A.D. inclusive. The Julian calendar is used
before 1752 and the Gregorian calendar after 1752. Output
is on the Teletype or high speed punch.

Minimum Hardware:
4K PDP-8, ASR33
Storage Requirement:
20-60, 200-1537
Source Language:
PAL III

Catalog: July 1973
