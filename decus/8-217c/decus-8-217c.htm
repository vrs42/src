UTIL
8-217C

Mathew Simon, Computer Applications, Inc., New York,
New York

UTIL enables manipulation of the assembled files that are
output on the TU-20 by DECUS NO. 8-217A and 8-217B.

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8 with two banks of core
storage, DF-32 Disk, TU-20
magnetic tape drive

Other Programs Needed:
PALM and PALR (DECUS NO.
8-217A and 8-217B)

Source Language:
PAL-D

Catalog:July 1973
