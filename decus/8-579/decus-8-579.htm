LISTIT
8-579

Geoffrey Chase, O.S.B., Portsmouth Abbey School,
Portsmouth, Rhode Island

LISTIT reads an ASCII paper tape, either 7 or 8-level code,
and prints its contents on the console TTY or DECwriter,
restoring tabs and ejecting pages in uniform length.

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8/E (F, M), TTY or DECwriter,
paper tape reader
Source Language:
PAL III

Catalog:July 1973
