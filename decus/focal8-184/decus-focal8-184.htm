Manpower
FOCAL8-184

C. C. Wilton-Davies, Royal Naval Physiological Laboratory,
Alverstoke, Gosport, Honts, England

This is an interactive program for allocating manpower between
a number of jobs with different deadlines. The costs of
different solutions may be compared, and options of overtime,
hiring and firing are available.

Minimum Hardware:
8K PDP-8
Other Programs Needed:
8K FOCAL
Source Language:
8K FOCAL '69
