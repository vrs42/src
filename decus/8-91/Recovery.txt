These binary I found at bitsavers was patched with the changes
specified in the change notice at the end of the write-up.

The change notice is actually wrong.  The routine referenced 
by location 3204 is not a subroutine, and should be the target 
of a JMP, not a JMS.

With these changes, the patched binary matches the checksum 
from the image on bitsavers.  These corrections have been 
made to decus-8-91-pb.

The files micro-8.{pal,lst,bin} are an annotated disassembly 
of the corrected binary.
