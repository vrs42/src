Advanced Averager Program (Rotterdam Version)
8-547

L.T.M.E. Hillegers and S. Miller, Medical Faculty,
Department of Anatomy, Rotterdam, The Netherlands

This is a modification of the LAB-8 Advanced Averager Program
(Paper Tape Version C. DEC-LB-U17/18C-LA) for one-
pass loading of Sections III, IV and V, including additional
functions used in neurophysiological research (oscilloscope
display of reflex conditioning curves).

Minimum Hardware:
4K PDP-8/L, AX08 Laboratory
Peripheral, TTY, Oscilloscope
Other Programs Needed:
RIM and BIN Loaders

Catalog:July 1973
