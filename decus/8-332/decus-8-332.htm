The Civil War Game
8-332

Ronald Servi, Lexington High School, Lexington,
Massachusetts

This program, created to fulfill a U. S. History assignment,
presents a three month picture of the Civil War in the form of
a report to the commander-in-chief of the Union Army. The
player decides which strategy, amount of weapons, men and
money are necessary. The computer, in addition to playing
for the South, also determines what action takes place during
the war.
