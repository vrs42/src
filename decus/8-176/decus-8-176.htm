PAL CHOP
8-176

Edward A. Taft, III, St. Mark's School, Southboro,
Massachusetts

PAL CHOP produces minimum-length copies of PAL source
tapes by removing all comments, tabs, multiple spaces,
and multiple carriage-return/line-feed's. It is especially
useful in facilitating the handling and storage of sections
of extremely large programs which have been debugged.

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8, 8/I, 8/L and
ASR-33. High-Speed reader
and punch optional.

Source Language:
PAL-D

Storage Requirement:
Program occupies 108-3668:
uses 4008-11778 as buffer

Execution time: I/O limited

Catalog:November 1969
