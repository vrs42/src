FOCAL8-150

Paul Fingerman, Department of Psychology, State University
of New York, Stony Brook, New York

This overlay is a modified version of DECUS NO. FOCAL8-1
which was an adaptation of Brady's random number generator
for FOCAL W. It does not replace it. This version was rewritten
for FOCAL-69 without extended functions. It is relocatable,
and can easily be adapted for use with extended
functions.

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8, ASR33
Other Programs Needed:
FOCAL-69
Storage Requirement:
668 locations, relocatable
Source Language:
PAL III

Catalog: July 1973
