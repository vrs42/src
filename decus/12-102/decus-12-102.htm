A Manual for the PDP-12 Operator
12-102
Author: Peter Hiscocks, Ryerson Polytechnical Institute, Toronto, Ontario, Canada
Most manuals are for the computer; this one is for the operator. Its purpose is to clarify operating procedures for the PDP-12 console, paper tape loaders, DIAL Operating System, Peripheral Interchange Program (PIP) and the special PDP-12 peripherals. It was written primarily for students.
Catalog: July 1974
