MTSAFE
8-205

John Alderman, Georgia Institute of Technology, Atlanta,
Georgia

MTSAFE is a TC-58 version of DECUS No. 8-120, Disk/DECtape
FAILSAFE. It is fairly self-explanatory and incorporates
additional messages for the operator to service the magtape.

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8 with TC-58 Magtape

Other Programs Needed:
Disk Monitor System (DEC-D8-SDAA)

Storage Requirement:
SA-0200; occupies 100-13778 and
uses 1400-21778 as buffer

Catalog: November 1969
