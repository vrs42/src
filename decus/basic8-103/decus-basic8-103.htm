Operating System: OS/8
Source Language: BASIC V3.21
Abstract: CARD is a comprehensive simulator for CARDIAC, a simplified machine language developed by Bell Telephone Laboratories for teaching elementary programming concepts. CARD, written in OS/8 BASIC, interprets and executes CARDIAC programs, while also providing editing, tracing and listing capabilities. Input comes from cards or keyboard, and output can be directed to the console or line printer. Fully interactive and diagnostic, it is easy to use and very useful in an educational environment. Complete instructions and four sample programs are provided. CARD assumes a VT-50 or VT-52 terminal and requires 16K words of memory.
