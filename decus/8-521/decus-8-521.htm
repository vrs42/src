A CLOCK
8-521

Klaus Lickteig, Technische Universitaet Berlin, Berlin,
Germany

This demonstration program will display a clock on the
oscilloscope of an AX08 A/D converter. After setting the
clock, the running clock will be displayed on the oscilloscope.
There are possibilities to regulate the clock during running.

Minimum Hardware:
4K PDP-8, AX08 A/D Converter,
ASR33
Storage Requirement:
Locations 0-2, 10, 20-57, 200-
4347
Source Language:
PAL III
