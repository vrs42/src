DECtape Handler (552 Control)
8-121

B. Eiben, III Physikalisches Institut der T. H. Aachen,
Aachen, West Germany

This program allows quick, controlled data-block transfers
between the PDP-8 and DECtape. It reads, writes and
searches in minimum time (interrupt mode), requires minimum
space (overlay-with last page BIN, RIM, DECSYS Loaders)
and occupies only two blocks on tape (block 0 = System,
block 1 = Return-System). It is protected against destruction
and gives, after the transfer, the status levels for testing
purposes. It is usuable as a Switch Register controlled program
or as a subroutine with or without interrupt, giving
the possibility of quick data storage, program shuffling and
overlay technique with PDP-8 and DECtape.

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8, DECtape 552 Control

Catalog: November 1969
