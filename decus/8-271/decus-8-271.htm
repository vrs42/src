LIP - LogicaI "if" package
8-271

Bryan D. Young, University Department of Medical Cardiology,
Royal Infirmary, Glasgow, Scotland

LIP enables "IF" statements involving floating point variables
to be written in a conventional manner within assembly
language programs. The logical relations available are:
GT(>), GE(>), EQ(=), LE(<), LT(<), NE(<>), logical AND,
logical OR.

Minimal Hardware:
PDP-8
Other Programs Needed:
3 word Floating Point Package
(Digital 8-5-S)
Storage Requirement:
4, 5400-5577
Source Language:
PAL-D

Catalog:July 1973
