Clock Calibration
8-614

Masashi Kamii, The Central Institute for Experimental
Animals, Nogawa, Kowasaki, Japan

Using CRT (RM503) and X'TAL-clock in an AX08 configuration
this program allows visible calibration of the RC-clock.

Minimum Hardware:
LAB 8/I (PDP-8/I and AX08
without XR, XC, XM option)
Source Language:
PAL III

Catalog:July 1973
