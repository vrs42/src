Four Word Floating Point Functions For MPS
8-737B

Robert H. Tedford, Digital Equipment Corporation,
Marlboro, Massachusetts

This function package was written for use with the Four Word
Floating Point for MP5 (DECUS 8-737A) and includes sub-
routines to evaluate square, square root, sine, cosine, arc-
tangent, natural logarithm and experimental Functions.

Minimum Hardware:
MP5
Other Programs Needed:
DECUS NO. 8-737A
Source Language:
PAL-8
Catalog: May 1975
