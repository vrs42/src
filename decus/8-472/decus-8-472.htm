PS8IN, PS8OUT
8-472

Hans Mees and Floor Anthoni, Medical Biological Laboratory,
T.N.O., Rijswijk, The Netherlands

Designed to make PS/8 input-output programming much easier,
the two modules perform automatic actions like calling the
COMMAND DECODER, opening and closing files, etc. A
JMS to PS8IN simply returns the next character, a JMS to
PS8OUT writes it away. The routines relieve the programmer
from the cumbersome PS/8 I/O programming, and they can be
used separately. (These two modules make PS/8 input-output
programming as easy as teletype input-output.
