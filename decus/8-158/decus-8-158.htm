AX08 Symbol Generator
8-158

D. Dyment, Digital Equipment of Canada, Ltd., Carleton
Place, Ontario, Canada

This subroutine may be called to display single characters or
a string of characters on the oscilloscope of an AX08 (LAB8)
system. Sixty different symbols, in addition to four special
"format" codes, are provided by the routine. Software control
of character scaling and "margins" on the display is provided.

Minimum Hardware:
LAB-8 with osci Iloscope

Source Language:
PAL

Storage Requirement:
22310 locations

Catalog:November 1969
