Readable High Speed Punch Copier
8-382

Nigel D. Chubbb, Collins Radio Company of United Kingdom,
Limited, Hounslow, Middlesex, United Kingdom

This program accepts alphanumeric characters from teletype
and outputs them in a readable form on a high speed punch.
On depression of ALTMODE key, a copy is made of tape
placed in high speed reader.

Minimum Hardware:
Basic PDP-8 with high speed
reader and punch, ASR33
Storage Requirement:
3 pages+
Source Language:
PAL III

Catalog: July 1973
