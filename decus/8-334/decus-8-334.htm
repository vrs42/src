KVEDIT
8-334

Edward Friedman and Evan Suits, Digital Equipment
Corporation, Maynard, Massachusetts

KVEDIT is the standard PS/8 Text Editor modified for scope
display. All normal editing commands are available as well
as three additional commands for display control.

Minimum Hardware:
8K PDP-8, DECtape or Disk,
KV8/I Graphics
Other Programs Needed:
PS/8 Operating System
Source Language:
PAL8

Catalog: July 1973
