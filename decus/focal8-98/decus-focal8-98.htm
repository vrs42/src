FOCAL PUNCH OVERLAY
FOCAL8-98

James J. Chang, Department of Chemistry, University of
California at Berkeley, Berkeley, California

FOCAL PUNCH OVERLAY permits the FOCAL user to use the
FOCAL "Write" command to punch out indirect programs on
the high speed punch. The overlay self-destructs after use and
must be reloaded to be used again.

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8, ASR33, high speed punch
Other Programs Needed:
FOCAL-69
Source Language:
PAL III

Catalog:July 1973
