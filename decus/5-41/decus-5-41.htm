Breakpoint
5-41

Arthur R. Miller, Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution,
Woods Hole, Massachusetts

This debugging routine has been reduced to a minimum
operation. It is a mobile routine which can operate around
any program that leaves an extra 30 cells of memory space.

Its function is to insert break points in any given location of
the program being debugged, and to hold the contents of
AC and Link. The programmer may examine any locations
desired and then continue to the next breakpoint. It is
presently located in 1408 -1708, but may be easily relocated.

Storage Requirement:
1408 -1708
