DECtape Reader Handler for PDP-12
12-189
Author: Gotz Romahn, Heinrich Hertz Institut, Berlin, Germany
This handler allows direct reading of DECtapes via the TC12F option. All OS/8 - V3 programs may be used.
Minimum Hardware: PDP-12 with TC12F option and EAE
Other Programs Needed: OS/8 BUILD
Restrictions: Possibly will not run on a very slow PDP-12 and a very fast tape drive
Source Language: PAL-8
Catalog: May 1975
