Binary Tape Splicer ASR 33/75A
8-281a

Ion Grove-White, University of Aberdeen, Aberdeen, Scotland
Revision by: P. Galen Lenhert, Vanderbilt University,
Nashville, Tennessee

This utility program punches a length of leader tape and halts.
It then transcribes binary tapes as they are fed in, without
interruption on the output tape. It finishes by punching a new
checksum and a length of trailer tape. Splicer tapes can be
read into memory using the binary loader.

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8, ASR33 or 75A High
Speed Punch and Reader
Source Language:
PAL

Catalog: July 1973
