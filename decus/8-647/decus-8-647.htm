FULMIX - Complete Permutation Program
8-647

Bradford Needham, South Salem High School, Salem, Oregon

Prints all the unique permutations of a given set of units,
using the minimum amount of paper (limit of 32 decimal
units, but can be easily increased). These units may be any
number of characters, and any number of units may be alike.

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8/L, TTY
Source Language:
Machine Language

Catalog:July 1974
