FOCAL Horserace for the PDP-8 (8K) Computer
FOCAL8-92

Gilbert S. Fair, Digital Equipment Corporation, Northbrook,
Illinois

This program simulates a horserace with 9 horses, using a
random number generator to produce different results for eoch
race run, and permitting 20 or so bets to be placed on each
race.

Minimum Hardware:
8K PDP-8, ASR33
Storage Requirement:
7000-8000 words (including
FOCAL)
Source Language:
FOCAL - 69

Catalog: July 1973
