Memory Halt - A PDP-5 Program to Store HALT in Most of
5,8-34
Memory

P. T. Brady, Department of Electrical Engineering, New
York University, New York City, New York

With Memory Halt and OPAK in memory, it is possible to
store HALT (7402) in the following memory locations: 0001
to 0005, 0007 to 6177, and 7402 to 7403.

Other Programs Needed:
OPAK (DECUS No. 5-2.1)

Catalog: November 1969
