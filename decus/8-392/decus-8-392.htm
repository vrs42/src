Vector-8
8-392

Richard Rothman, Groton School, Groton, Massachusetts

Vector-8, a new programming language, is designed to allow
the user of a PDP-8 with 8K and a DF32 Disk to take full
advantage of the hardware. It offers 33 functions, 15 opera-
tors and 22 commands.

Minimum Hardware:
8K PDP-8, DF32 Disk
Source Language:
PAL-10

Catalog: July 1973
