CFI - Continued Fraction Inversion
8-568

Andres T. Siy, Capitol Institute of Technology, Kensington,
Maryland

Program to convert a real continued fraction into a rational
function.

A sample of evaluating electric network transfer function is
given.

Minimum Hardware:
8K PDP-8/I, ASR33, HSR/P
Other Programs Needed:
8K FORTRAN Compiler, 8K SABR
Assembler, 8 K Linking Loader,
User's Program
Source Language:
8K FORTRAN

Catalog:July 1973
