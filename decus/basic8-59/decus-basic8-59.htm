STORM3BASIC8-59
Submitted by: Bradford A. Morse
Abstract: This is a program written in BASIC on Edusystem-20, to simulate formations of clouds, rain storms, and the breakup of the clouds after the rain. It requires only that you can type it into the computer (PDP-8/E), and type the word "RUN". It will take it from there. The program works entirely with random numbers and simulates buildups and breakdowns of clouds by printing progress reports by the hour until the storm is over. After the storm a complete description of the storm's actions are printed out.
