MMMS (Calculation of Minimum, Mean, Maximum and
8-320
Standard Deviation)

J. N. R. Jeffers
Submitted by: A. J. P. Gore, The Nature Conservancy,
Lancashire, England

This program calculates the minimum, arithmetic mean,
maximum and standard deviation of any number of sets of up
to 20 variables, presented in a standard order. It is intended
as a simple method of summarizing multi-variate data, and is
used as an auxiliary program for other multivariate programs,
for example, the CCMP program.

Minimum Hardware:
4K PDP-8/I, DF32 Disk, High
Speed Reader
