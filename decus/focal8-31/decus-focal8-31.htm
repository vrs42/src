Sines; Factors; Figure Eight; Right Triangle Solutions
FOCAL8-31

Submitted by: Digital Equipment Corporation, Maynard,
Massachusetts

Sines - Plotting several variables using sine functions.

Factors - Determines whether a number is prime or gives its
factors.

Figure Eight - Will plot various sizes of figure eights by
means or residuals. This is represented by a simple fourth
order equation.

Right Triangle Solutions - Applies trigonometry relations and
the Pythagorean theorem for a right triangle.

Catalog: July 1973
