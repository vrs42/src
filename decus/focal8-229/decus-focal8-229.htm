H-800 Wiring Diagrams
FOCAL8-229

Thomas J. Ford, White Mountains Regional High School,
Whitefield, New Hampshire

A DEC H-800-W connector is diagrammed and wire lists, pin
diagrams and change orders randomly prepared for it. A
second program handles real cases for one connector.

Minimum Hardware:
4K PDP-8, ASR33
Other Programs Needed:
FOCAL 5/69 (DECUS NO.
FOCAL8-52a)
Restrictions:
Will cause trouble if used with
other versions of FOCAL
Source Language:
FOCAL 5/69

Catalog: July 1973
