BIGCHARS
12-161
Author: Roy D. Eassa, Jamesville-DeWitt Central Schools, DeWitt, New York
BIGCHARS displays 5 X 7 characters on the VR12 scope and prints enlarged patterns on the LS8E line printer. All printing teletype characters are acceptable.
Minimum Hardware: PDP-12, VR12 Scope, ASR33, LSBE
Other Programs Needed: PAL12A (DECUS NO. 12-77)
Storage Requirement: 4K
Source Language: PAL12A
Catalog: July 1974
