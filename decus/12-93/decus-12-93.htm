TRANS
12-93
Author: Arnold Gershon
Submitted by: Professor Se Jeung Oh, City College, New York
Will translate a BIN paper tape to an octal Source paper tape. The octal source can then be assembled together with other routines. In addition, the translation will satisfy any curiosity about what a particular BIN tape contains.
Minimum Hardware: PDP-12A
Storage Requirement: 738 words
Source Language: DIAL-V2
Catalog: July 1974
