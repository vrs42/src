VR12 SCOPE HANDLER FOR OS/8
12-81
Author: Mario DeNobili et al
Submitted by: Stanley Rabinowitz, Digital Equipment Corporation, Maynard, Massachusetts
This is a two-page handler for the PDP-12 VR12 scope. Display freezes when scope fills up. You go to next scope page by typing any character. Very useful with PIP.
Minimum Hardware: PDP-12, PS/8 configuration
Other Programs Needed: PS/8 system
Storage Requirement: 2 pages
Source Language: PAL-8
Catalog: July 1974
