DECI: A Subroutine to Output in Decimal
8-214

John M. Martin, University of California, Psychobiology
Department, Irvine, California

This routine will type the decimal equivalent of the octal
number in the accumulator on the Teletype from -2048 to
+2047 with zero suppression.

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8; ASR-33

Storage Requirement:
9210

Source Language:
PAL III

Catalog: July 1973
