DECtape Programming System (552 & TC01 Controls)
5,8-64

Submitted by: DEC Software Services Group, Digital
Equipment Corporation, Maynard, Massachusetts

This program provides rapid access to DEC software and
utilizes routines through the use of DECtape. Programs
may be stored, edited, assembled, listed, or executed
without reliance upon paper tape.

May be used with both TC01 and 552 DEC tape Controls.
When requesting this program please specify which version.

Catalog: November 1969
