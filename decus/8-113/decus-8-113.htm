Conversion of Frieden (EIA) to ASCII
8-113

John F. Puterbaugh, Continental Aviation and Engineering
Corporation, Toledo,' Ohio

This program will translate tapes prepared on a Friden Flexo-
writer (EIA) into ASCII for direct assembly, further editing,
or feeding into the FORTRAN program. Alphabetic characters
may be in either upper or lower case. The program uses a table
lookup and comparison with the negative complement of the
EIA character, then outputs the corresponding ASCII character.

Note: This is not Frieden FIO-DEC.

Storage Requirement:
2138 including 2 autoindex registers

Catalog:November 1969
