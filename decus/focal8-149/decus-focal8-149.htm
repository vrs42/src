Checkers

Paul M. Klinkman, North Smithfield Jr. Sr. High School,
Woonsocket, Rhode ls land

The computer plays a slightly modified version of checkers
using this program. The checkers never land on 32 spaces.
This saves 32 variables. Because of the strange nature of the
board, checkers can't go off one side of the board to the other
side.

Restrictions:
Unable to handle multiple jumps
