OS/8 DIBILD - Revised
12-131
Author: John C. Alderman
Revised by: Mark F. Lewis, Civil Aeromedical Institute, Federal Aviation Administration, Oklahoma City, Oklahoma
OS/8 DIBILD is a PS/8 or OS/8 utility program to restore directories that have been overwritten. This version is a revision of John Alderman's original program (DECUS NO. 8-599), revised to correct coding that was illegal on straight (or 'classic') PDP-8's. One option has been added.
Minimum Hardware: Any PS/8 or OS/8 installation with LINCtape
Source Language: PAL-8
Catalog: July 1974
