$CORR. (FOCAL Version)
12-146
Author: Jim Hoare, Lakeshore Psychiatric Hospital, Toronto, Ontario, Canada
This program computes the means and standard deviations and the matrix of correlation coefficients for multivariate data.

For the long version it will take a little time for the results. (Example: 40 minutes for 30 variables by 15 subjects.)

Minimum Hardware: PDP-12 with TTY
Source Language: FOCAL-12
Catalog: July 1974
