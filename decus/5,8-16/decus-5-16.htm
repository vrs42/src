Paper Tape Duplicator for the PDP-5
5-16

Henry Burkhardt, Digital Equipment Corporation, Maynard,
Massachusetts

The Paper Tape Duplicator for the PDP-5 is a single-buffered
read and punch program utilizing the program interrupt. It
computes a character count and checksum for each tape and
compares with checks at the end of the tape. Checks are
also computed and compared during punching.

Catalog:November 1969
