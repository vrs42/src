BLACKJACK for FOCAL, 1969
FOCAL8-267

Jeffrey Scott, 8604 Bunnell Drive, Potomac, Maryland

This program plays Blackjack with a user. The computer acts
as dealer and computes all winnings and losses. After a full
deck of 52 different cards is dealt by the dealer, the
teletype bell rings to show that a new deck has been started.
The computer usually wins, but it is not a perfect player.

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8
Other Programs Needed:
FOCAL, 1969 (DEC-08-AJAE-PB)
Source Language:
FOCAL, 1969
