There are two versions of the CBL loader (cbl5 
and cbl8).  One runs only on the PDP-5, and the 
other only on the PDP-8.  The only actual change 
is the ordering of instructions around a "7417"
instruction, which skips on the PDP-5 and not on 
the PDP-8.  I don't know why an ordering that
would run on both machines was not chosen.

The other thing I have placed here is a patch 
for some version of the PAL assembler.  I also 
found a corrupted CBL binary of the patched 
assembler, which I have attempted to repair 
here.

With the exception of decus-5-26.pal (the patch),
the sources here are just disassemblies of the 
binaries.
