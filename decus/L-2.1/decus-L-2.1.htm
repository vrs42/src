"Clock 1" for LINC and "Clock 8" for LINC-8
L-2.1
Author: Prof. P. Finkenzeller, University of Erlangen, Erlangen, West Germany
"Clock 1" and "Clock 8" are demonstration programs for the LINC and LINC-8 respectively. The programs also check for the position of sense switch 0. If the switch is down, the clock runs normal (keeping time); switch up and the clock will run fast (not keeping time).
Catalog: November 1969
