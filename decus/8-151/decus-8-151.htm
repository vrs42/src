On-Line TIC-TAC-TOE
8-151

Richard B. Rothman, Groton School, Groton, Connecticut

This program plays the game of TIC-TAC-TOE with the
user. By means of a previously stored algorithm, it selects
the best move for any given situation. Conversation and
ultimate defeat is is via the Teletype.

Source Language:
PAL
