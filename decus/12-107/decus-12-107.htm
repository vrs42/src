AVUPTOB, AVUPTOBS
12-107
Author: A. M. Halliday, Medical Research Council, National Hospital, London, United Kingdom
A flexible multichannel averaging program for evoked responses, allowing choice of number of sweeps, channels and sampling rate via the teletype. Averaged data can be stored or retrieved from tape (1600 blocks with AVUPTOBS) and plotted out. Display monitors running sum or current input. The averaging sweep is triggered by an external pulse on the chosen sync input.
Minimum Hardware: 8K PDP-12A, KW12, Houston Complot DPT, XY plotter
Other Programs Needed: LAP6-DTAL
Restrictions: Up to 8 channels. Maximum sampling rate: 10 Kcs/number of channels chosen
Source Language: LAP6-DIAL
Catalog: July 1974
