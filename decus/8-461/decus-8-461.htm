COPY10 - PDP-10 DECtape Program for the PDP-8
8-461

Frank J. Nagy, Carnegie Mellon University, Pittsburgh,
Pennsylvania

COPY10 reads and writes files between PDP-8 devices (disk,
DECtape, paper tape) and a PDP-10 DECtape. ASCII files
can be read from or written to the PDP-10 DECtape. BIN files
(generated by PAL-10) can also be read. Program also reads
PDP-10 ASCII paper tapes.

Minimum Hardware:
8K PDP-8, TC01 DECtape Control
with 2 DECtape drives
Other Programs Needed:
Disk/DECtape Monitor System
Source language: PAL-10

Catalog:February 1972
