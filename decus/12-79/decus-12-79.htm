Modified ADTAPE
12-79
Author: William E. Hatcher, III, Veteran's Administration Center, Temple, Texas
This patch to ADTAPE will permit ADTAPE to store data on a LINCtape of 896 (1600(8)) standard blocks. Data files may begin in any block and may be continuous over blocks 511 and 512 (777(8) and 1OOO(8)). The experiment parameters can be stored in any of the 896 blocks.
Minimum Hardware: PDP-12/2O
Other Programs Needed: ADTAPE (DEC-12-SE2E)
Storage Requirement: Same as ADTAPE plus locs 7600:- 7606, 7500-7507, 7700-7706
Source Language: LAP6
Catalog: July 1974
