Self-Starting PS/8 Loader
8-516

F. J. Meijer, University of Amsterdam, Amsterdam,
The Netherlands

This program produces on paper tape a self-starting PS/8
loader for the PDP-8/E with TD8-E DECtape

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8/E with TD8-EM
Other Programs Needed:
PAL Assembler
Storage Requirement:
One Page
Source Language:
PAL

Catalog: July 1973
