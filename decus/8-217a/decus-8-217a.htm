PALR
8-217A

Mathew Simon, Computer Applications, Inc., New York,
New York

PALR will determine whether or not the output from the Tele-
type is an error message. If no error occurs, the output is
directed to a TU-20 magnetic tape. Symbols are extended
into bank one; not on the disk.

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8 with two banks of core
storage, DF-32 Disk, TU-20
magnetic tape drive

Other Programs Needed:
PAL-D

Catalog:July 1973
