Modifications to TSS/8 FOCAL
FOCAL8-157

Allon B. Wilson, Computer Applications Associates, Houston,
Texas

TSS/8 FOCAL modified to provide two new capabilities: Use
of arithmetic expressions as targets in branching statements,
and disk storage of FOCAL programs. The patches occupy the
core areas assigned to the high speed paper tape reader routines,
the library command, and the binary loader in standard
4K FOCAL-69, hence, no text or pushdown space in TSS/8
FOCAL is sacrificed with the inclusion of these features.

Minimum Hardware:
TSS/8
Source Language:
FOCAL-69

Catalog:July 1973
