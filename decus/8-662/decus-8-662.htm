UNDEFSYBLIST - Undefined Symbol List
8-662

Roger Geffen, Data Research Associates, Wayland,
Massachusetts

This patch, based on the "Alpha List" program by W. F.
Haygood, Jr. , causes MACRO-8 to list any undefined
symbols at the end of pass l. Space for the patch is made by
slightly reducing the size of the HSR buffer.

Other Programs Needed:
MACRO-8
Source Language:
PAL

Catalog: July 1974
