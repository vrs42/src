XPIP8: PDP-12 DECtape PIP
12-149
Author: Douglas E. Wrege, Georgia Institute of Technology, Atlanta, Georgia
XPIP8 is a special version of PS/8 PIP for PDP-12 owners with the TC12-F option. XPIP8 allows direct reading and writing of PS/8-OS/8 DECtapes mounted on any LINCtape drive on a PDP-12. It will only work on those machines equipped with the TC12F option.
Minimum Hardware: 8K, TC12-F option
Other Programs Needed: PS/8, OS/8 or DECsystem-8 (DECUS NO. 8-646)
Source Language: PAL
Catalog: July 1974
