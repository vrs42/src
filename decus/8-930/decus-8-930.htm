FOCAL Yahtzee, Version: June 1982
8-930

Author: Goedon S. Hlavenka, United States Navy

Operating System: Independent Source Language: FOCAL Memory
Required: 8K Other Software Required: Requires 8K overlay to FOCAL.
Keywords: Games

Abstract: This program rolls dice and keeps score for a one-player game of
Yahtzee. It requires only 8K FOCAL and a teletype. 'HELP' functions are
included to assist the player in communicating with the program. Dice are
checked during scoring to prevent cheating, however the program is
annotated and organized to facilitate modification to suit your whims. A
header at the start of each turn provides information on your current
status, and a complete scorecard is typed out at the end of the game.

Restrictions: One player only.

Media Service Charge Code: Listing (BA)
Catalog: June 1985
