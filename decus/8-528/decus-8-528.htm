TIC-TAC-TOE: Modifications to TIC 5/8, DECUS NO. 8-173
8-528

Klaus Lickteig, Technische Universitaet Berlin, Berlin,
Germany

This modification to TIC 5/8 makes it possible to run TIC 5/8
on a LAB-8 System and to play TIC-TAC-TOE.

Minimum Hardware:
4K PDP-8/I or 8/E with AX08
A/D Converter and ASR33
Other Programs Needed:
TIC 5/8 (DECUS NO. 8-173)
Storage Requirement:
With TIC 5/8: 1-3, 33-3053
Source Language:
PAL III

Catalog: July 1973
