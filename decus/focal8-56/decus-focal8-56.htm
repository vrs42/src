Merchandise Price Tags
FOCAL8-56

Thomas J. Ford, White Mountains Regional High School,
Whitefield, New Hampshire

This program represents a preliminary effort to use the FOCAL
language for the solution of a business oriented problem.
With minimum input, the program will generate merchandise
price tags, including the merchant's per-package-cost in a
convenient code.

Source Language:
FOCAL - 69

Catalog: July 1973
