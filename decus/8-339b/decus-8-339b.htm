Time Interval Histogram Program
8-339B

Charles P. Merrill, Digital Equipment Corporation,
Maynard, Massachusetts

Records the time between events occurring on Schmitt trigger
2 of the AX08. The display is a frequency distribution show-
ing the duration of the interval vs. its frequency of occur-
rence.

Minimum Hardware:
LAB-8 (PDP-8/I or 8/L with
AX08)
Other Programs Needed:
Write-up - DECUS NO. 8-339A
Storage Requirement:
4K
Source Language:
PAL III

Catalog: July 1973
