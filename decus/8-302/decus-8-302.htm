Overlay Modifications to the Floating Point System Packages,
8-302
DEC-08-YQYA

Peter l. Walton
Submitted by: Dr. William S. Yamamoto, School of Medicine,
University of Pennsylvania, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania

This is an expansion of the present 4 floating-point packages
into 24 different packages having capabilities of basic func-
tions, extended functions, output controller, output formatter
with 4 I/O functions, extended memory referencing ability
and EAE utilization. Also an error in the present normaliza-
tion routine is corrected.

Minimum Hardware:
8K PDP-8, EAE
Other Programs Needed:
DEC-08-YQYA
Source Language:
MACRO-8

Catalog: July 1973
