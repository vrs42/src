GRFIT, A Simple Least Squares Routine
8-483

R. C. Gross, Eastman Kodak Company, Rochester, New York

This program accepts data for the arrays x and y where there
is scatter in the y array. It calculates the best least sguares
straight line and gives standard error estimates. A subroutine
version that is essentially the same is also included.

Minimum Hardware:
8K PDP-8, 1 DECtape
Other Programs Needed:
PS/8 Operating System and
FORTRAN
Miscellaneous:
See also FOCAL8-209
Source Language:
PS/8 FORTRAN
