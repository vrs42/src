Page Routine
8-184

F. Weil, Automatic Control Engineering, Ltd., Kent,
England

This program will arrange listings in page lengths and
sequentially number the pages.

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8 with ASR-33

Source Language:
PAL III

Storage Requirement:
Approximately 2008 words

Restrictions:
Maximum of 99 pages per listing.

Catalog: November 1969
