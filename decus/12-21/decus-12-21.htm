Modified MAGSPY
12-21
Author: Lawrence Moss, University of Vermont, College of Medicine, Burlington, Vermont
MAGSPY is in the DEMO monitor. Addition of an octal display of data, CR to restart DIAL, startup with the request for block number and unit, and display movement controlled by knob 4 make this modification different.
Minimum Hardware: PDP-12A, (LINCtapes, A/D scope)
Source Language: DIAL - patched
Catalog: July 1974
