LSQ Stern-Volmer: Least Squares Treatment of the General
FOCAL8-221
Stern-Volmer Equation

Dr. James E. Gano and Dr. H, Bradford Thompson, University
of Toledo, Toledo, Ohio

The Stern-Volmer Equation, often utilized by photochemists
to treat data, in its most general form (reactive and quench-
able triplet and singlet states) is processed by an iterative
least squares approach applicable to such nonlinear equations.
