CR8/I Overlay for PAL III Assembler
8-225

Robert A. Lammert, Digital Equipment Corporation,
Northbrook, Illinois

This overlay allows source input for the PAL III Assembler to
come from the CR8/I card reader. The overlay precludes
input from either High Speed paper tape reader or TTY.

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8, KSR-33 and CR8/I card
reader

Other Programs Needed:
PAL-III

Source Language:
PAL-III

Catalog: July 1973
