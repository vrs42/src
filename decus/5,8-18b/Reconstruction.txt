decus-5,8-18b appears to be simply decus-5,8-18a 
with a patch to paginate the output.  This is 
fortunate, as decus-5,8-18a has a listing in the 
write-up, and the available decus-5,8-18b binaries 
had checksum errors.  This version is based on 
the retyped decus-5,8-18a, with the patch manually 
added based on disassembly of the repaired binary.
The repaired binary has also been made available 
as decus-5-18b.bin.
