Galactic Coordinates
8-501

A. Moses, Computer Applications Engineering Company,
El Paso, Texas

This program consists of two parts: the first converts star
positions from astronomical coordinates (right ascension and
declination) to galactic coordinates (latitute and longitude);
the other converts in the reverse direction.

Minimum Hardware:
4K PDP-8L, TTY
Other Programs Needed:
4K FORTRAN Compiler and
Operating System (DEC-08-AFCO)
Source Language:
4K FORTRAN

Catalog:July 1973
