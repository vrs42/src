CURFIT
FOCAL8-63

Donald L. Shirer, Valparaiso University, Valparaiso, Indiana

CURFIT is a program written in the FOCAL language which
fits weighted or unweighted data to a straight line on a
Cartesian, log-log or semilog graph. It calculates the slope
and intercept of the line, the standard error in these values,
plus other measures of the "goodness" of fit. Values may be
added or deleted from the data list easily, and there is no
limit to the number of sample data pairs.

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8, ASR33
Source Language:
FOCAL

Catalog: July 1973
