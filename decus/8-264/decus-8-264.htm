CLOK - AX08 RC Clock or External Clock Frequency or
8-264
Period Measurement

Andre Laviron, INSERM, Hospital Neurologique, Lyon,
France

By option of the switch register this program will allow either
Frequency or Period measurement, normal RC clock rate, RC
clock slowed by 8, RC clock or External clock. Frequencey
or Period is typed on the ASR33 every 2 seconds. Precision:
1/8000.

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8/I, AX08, ASR33
Other Programs Needed:
Floating Point Package #2
Source Language:
PAL

Catalog:July 1973
