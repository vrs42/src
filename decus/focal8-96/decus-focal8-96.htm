Statistics - Standard Deviation
FOCAL8-96

E. W. Coleman, Picker X-Ray Manufacturing
Submitted by: E. F. Steinfeld, Digital Equipment
Corporation, Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania

Accepts any number of data values from the keyboard or from
paper tape, computes and prints out mean, variance, standard
deviation, coefficient of variation, maximum data value,
minimum data value, and number of data points.

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8, ASR33 (high speed reader
optional)
Source Language:
FOCAL 8/68

Catalog: July 1973
