8K FOCAL Display
FOCAL8-154

Floor Anthoni, Medical Biological Laboratory, National
Defence Research Council TNO, The Netherlands

In computer-graphics it is necessary to be able to display both
lines and characters for a good picture. This program will
provide these possibilities for the experienced FOCAL user.
Two new functions (FX and FDIS) transform information from
the FOCAL program to the display routine.

Minimum Hardware:
8K PDP-8, KV 8/I interface

Catalog: July 1973
