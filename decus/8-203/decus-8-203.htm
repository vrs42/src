used ih conjunction with (DECUS No. 8-202).

4K PDP-8 with a Houston
Instrument Complot Plotter Model
6650, DP-1-1 or equivalent

2, 10, 11, 12, 20-167, 200-3654,
4000-7777 is reserved for storage

Restriction:
When used in conjunction with
PLOT (DECUS No. 8-202) extended
memory is required.
