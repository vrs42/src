Teletype Input-Output Package
8-285

Garth Peterson, Institute of Atmospheric Sciences, South
Dakota School of Mines and Technology, Rapid City, South
Dakota

This is a teletype control package containing subroutines for
single-character input and output and for output of packed
and open text. These subroutines are Single-field oriented,
but provide for interrupt-enabled and disabled operation.

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8 with Teletype
Storage Requirement:
One memory page
Restrictions:
Modification required for PDP-8/S
{Described in write-up)
Source Language:
PAL-D
