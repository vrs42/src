Weight Flow; Filter Design; Ohm's Law

Submitted by: Digital Equipment Corporation, Maynard,
Massachusetts

Column Width - Used for typesetting calculations

Traverse - A civil engineering and surveying application to
compute a closed traverse given bearings and distances.

Least Square "Linear Fit" - Finds slope and Y-intercepts for
the equation Y=MX+B given a set of data observations.

Nozzle Weight Flow - Checks inlet pressure ratio and cal-
culates the weight flow through the nozzle (from Hamilton
Standard).

Filter Design - Plots filter output as well as numerical
answers to a digital filter design problem.

Ohm's Law - Computes Ohm's Law.
