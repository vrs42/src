AVERAGER
12-84
Author: Richard W. Reeder, State University of New York, Stony Brook, New York
Used to simultaneously average 5 channels of analog data such as EEG, ECG, etc. and store the average on LINCtape.
Minimum Hardware: 8K, EAE, KW12A, TU55 (2) A/D
Storage Requirement: 0-2000, 4000-7777; field 1 0000-7777
Source Language: DIAL
Catalog: July 1974
