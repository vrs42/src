Bowling League Tabulator, Version: May 1974
8-716

Author: Philip Bujalski

Keywords: Sports

Abstract: This program automates the tabulation of a bowling league for
any amount of teams with any amount of bowlers on the teams. For each
bowler, total pinfall, total games, average, high games, low game, and high
triple are calculated.

Restrictions: Not compatible with OS/8 BASIC.
