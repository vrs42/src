Computer Programs in Use in the Water Qualities Division,
FOCAL8-159A
Vol. 1

Dr. Adrion Demayo, Water Quality Division, Department of
Fisheries and Forestry, Ottawa, Ontario, Canada

This is a booklet containing programs AD0001 through AD0008.
The first four programs are for Least Square Fit to Various
Types of Polynomial Expressions; three programs are for Least
Square Fit to a Linear Expression with Two or Three Variables
and the last program is for Solutions of a Polynomial Equation.

Catalog: July 1973
