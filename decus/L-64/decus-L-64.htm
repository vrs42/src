A Pseudo Random Number Generator for the LINC-8
L-64
Author: M. F. Lewis, W. M. Webber, J. E. Doyle, Federal Aviation Administration, Oklahoma City, Oklahoma
This progiam is the LINC-8 version of DECUS No. 5-25. It simulates on the LINC the 2's complement arithmetic of the PDP-5 routine, but returns with the equivalent LINC number in the accumulator.
Source Language: LAP6A
Storage Requirement: 20-247(8) locations Execution Time: less than 0.5 msec.
Catalog: November 1969
