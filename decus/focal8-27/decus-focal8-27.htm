Δ - Y Complex - This program does a DELTA-WYE trans-
formation for A-C circuits.
Y - Δ Complex - This program does a WYE-DELTA trans-
formation for A-C circuits

Series Resonant Circuit Analvsis - This program computes
resonant frequency, bandwidth, Q, and values of inductive
and capacitive reactance of resonance for a given R-L-C
series circuits. It also produces, upon request, a table of
impedance and phase angle for 10 points each side of the
resonant frequency. Distance between points is determined
by a user inputted DELTA-F.
