There are three listing routines described in this package:
TIDY - Makes neat listings of PAL III source programs.
LISTER - A SNOBOL program which is slower than TIDY
but gives page numbering, headings and storage locations.
SNOLST - A SNOBOL program similar to LISTER, which
makes neat listings of POLY SNOBOL source programs.

Minimum Hardware:
4K PDP-8, ASR33
Other Programs Needed:
SNOLST and LISTER require
POLYSNOBOL, TIDY requires
PAL III, RL Monitor
Source Language:
POLY SNOBOL and PAL III

Catalog:July 1973
