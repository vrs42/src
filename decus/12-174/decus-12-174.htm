CLOCK: Digital Clock with Westminster Chimes
12-174
Author: Michael F. LubozynskI, Vanderbilt Medical School, Nashville, Tennessee
This is a software digital clock written for a PDP-12 with the extended arithmetic element (EAE). It displays the current time, counting the line frequency on the KW12 clock control Input 1, and chimes on the quarter hour. The chimes are completely modifiable and consist of a series of musical notes played on the computer speaker. The "clock" also chimes the hours.
Source Language: DIAL
Catalog: December 1974
