COMBO
8-551

William R. Anderson, Jr. 2700 Virginia Avenue, N. W.,
Washington, D. C.

COMBO was developed to give the 8K user of PAL III the
ability to call up PAL from the Editor. The user can lock the
panel, and with the Switch Register and keyboard, call up
PAL and transfer Editor's buffer to PAL without the use of
lengthy paper tape operations.

Minimum Hardware:
8K PDP-8
Other Programs Needed:
BIN Loader, PAL III, Symbol
Editor
Source Language:
PAL III

Catalog:July 1973
