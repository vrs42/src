Amplitude Distribution
8-243

H. D. Schenk, Deutsche Forschungs und Versuchsanstalt fur
Luft und Raumfahrt E. V., Flughafen, Germcmy

This program calculates the amplitude distribution of a set of
points which are given on tape or are typed cm the teletype.
The output is via two D/A Converters. The mean, the variance
and the standard deviation are typed on the teleprinter.

Minimum Hardware:
4K PDP-8, ASR-33, PC01,
2 D/A Converters (No. 55, 56)
Other Programs Needed:
Floating Point Package No. 3
(DEC-8-5-S)
Source Language:
MACRO-8

Catalog:July 1973
