Keyboard Readable Punch
FOCAL8-87

Thomas J. Ford, White Mountains Regional High School,
Whitefield, New Hampshire

This program should prove useful to users of paper tape who
also have limited core storage. The routine operates manually
from the TTY keyboard (LOCAL) to punch readable
characters. Using this procedure, the leader portion of the
paper tape can be perforated in a meaningful way for tape
identification.

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8, ASR33
Source Language:
FOCAL 5/69 (DECUS NO.
FOCAL8-52)
