Blackjack
FOCAL8-75

Jerry D. Burns, Exotic Environments Laboratory, Arizona
State University, Tempe, Arizona

A modified Blackjack game, written in FOCAL. The game
contains standard Blackjack payoffs as well as a "double
down" option on hands of 10 or 11 .

Minimum Hardware:
4K PDP-8
Source Language:
FOCAL 8/68

Catalog: July 1973
