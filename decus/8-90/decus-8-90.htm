Histogram on Teletype
8-90

J. B. Levin, University of Arizona, Tucson, Arizona.

This routine plots histograms on the Teletype when there is
no CRT display available or a means of making a permanent
copy of a CRT display. Input to the routine consists of a
vertical scaling factor, the size of the table to be plotted
(limited only by the size of the Teletype print line), the
starting address of two core areas: one containing the data
to be plotted, and one for use as temporary storage by the
machine.

Storage Requirement:
12810 words plus tables

Catalog: November 1969
