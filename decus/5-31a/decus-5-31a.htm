FORPLOT: FORTRAN Plotting Program for PDP-5
5-31a

Jerome Feder, Department of Electrical Engineering,
New York University, New York City, New York

FORPLOT is a general-purpose plotting program for the PDP-5
computer in conjunction with the CalComp 560 Plotter. It
is self-contained and occupies memory locations 00008 up
to 41778, FORPLOT accepts decimal data inputted on paper
tape in either fixed or floating point formats. Formats can
be mixed at will. PDP-5 FORTRAN output tapes are acceptable
directly and any comment on these are filtered out.

Storage Requirement:
0000-41778

Catalog: November 1969
