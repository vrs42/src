OCTPUNCH
12-91
Author: Arnold Gershon
Submitted by: Professor Se Jeung Oh, City College, New York
Program will punch a source paper tape containing the octal equivalent of a specified section of core. This tape can then be assembled at a later time faster than its original source could be and without any conflicting symbols which the original might have contained.
Minimum Hardware: PDP-12A
Storage Requirement: 143(8) words
Source Language: DIAL-V2
Catalog: July 1974
