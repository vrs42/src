A Programmed Associative Multichannel Analyzer
8-65

G. C. Best, Atomic Energy Research Establishment,
Harwell, England

The program describes the use of a small compluter as an
associative analyzer with special reference to the PDP-8.
The advantages and limitations of the method are discussed
in the write-up, and general program algorithms are pre
sented.
