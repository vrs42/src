Square Root Patch for DEC-8-5-S
8-157

A. K. Head, C.S.I.R.O., University of Melbourne,
Parkville, Victoria, Australia

This program is a patch to standard SQRT routine (DEC-8-5-S).
It is a shorter and faster way of giving exact roots of exact
squares.
