8-722
Author: Philip Hunt, Middletown TWP. High School, Middletown, New Jersey
This program when loaded and started at 00200 will accept data, either Binary or ASCII from the high speed reader, a character at a time and send it out to the low speed punch on the ASR33 teletype.
Other Programs Needed: Bootstrap Loader
Source Language: PAL-D
Catalog: December 1974
