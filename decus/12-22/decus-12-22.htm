PLOTFFT
12-22
Author: J. R. Mason, U.C.L.A. Brain Research Institute, Los Angeles, California
PLOTFFT reads the LINCtape created by the CFFT program (DECUS NO. 12-23) and plots a graph of the auto spectra on a digital plotter.
Minimum Hardware: PDP-12 with 8-K memory, LINCtapes,
Digital Plotter (0.01 in/step)
Source Language: LAP6
Catalog: July 1974
