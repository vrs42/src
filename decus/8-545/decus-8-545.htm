PIF (Program Interrupt Facility for 3 TTY's)
8-545

Gary R, Garber, Laurence High School North, Trenton,
New Jersey

This is a one page utility or demonstration program that
recognizes CR/LF and CTRL/TAB on an interrupt basis, It
was originally designed to facilitate easier typing of programs,
but can be expanded to almost any use on an interrupt
basis,

Minimum Hardware:
4K PDP-8, 1 to 3 TTY's
Source Language:
PAL III

Catalog: July 1973
