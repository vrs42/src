Space War
8-395

Evan Suits, Digital Equipment Corporation, Maynard,

The classic game of Intergalactic Death and Destruction on a
LAB-8. Two players vie with ships in space for control of the
Universe. The ships may be controlled from the Switch
Register or from the AX08 front panel Blue Ribbon Connector.
