CBL2BN and BN2CBL
8-26B.1

David M. Kristol, University of Pennsylvania, Philadelphia,
Pennsylvania

CBL2BN is a short utility program which converts paper tape
in CBL format to BIN and BN2CBL converts paper tape from
BIN to CBL format. It offers high or low speed I/O and
proper punching of field characters.

Program Language: PDPMAP - (DECUS No. 8-166)

Storage Requirement:
3008 and 2008 Buffer; 4008 and
2008 Buffer

Catalog:November 1969
