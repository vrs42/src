4-Digit, 12-Bit Word Practice
FOCAL8-46

Thomas Ford, White Mountains Regional High School,
Whitefield, New Hampshire

This program will drill the student in interpreting lamp pat-
terns on console of PDP-8 computer. Lamp patterns are
presented, and then the user types in the correct octal nota-
tion. A tally is kept of the users' responses.

Minimum Hardware:
8K PDP-8
Source Language:
FOCAL-69

Catalog:July 1973
