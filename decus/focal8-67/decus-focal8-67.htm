T-Test
FOCAL8-67

Jerry D. Burns, Exotic Environments Laboratory, Arizona
State University, Tempe, Arizona

This program is designed to calculate students' T- ratio for
independent samples. The output format gives sample means
and variances, standard error of the mean difference, the
value of t, and the number of degrees of freedom upon which
t is distributed.

Minimum Hardware:
4K PDP-8
Source Language:
FOCAL 8/68

Catalog: July 1973
