BINDEC: Binary-to-Decimal Conversion
L-50
Author: Dennis J. Nichols, University of Wisconsin, Madison, Wisconsin
The BINDEC collection of conversion routines provides a standardized set of subroutines for doing various types of conversion from binary integers to decimal keyboard codes. The resulting LINCkeyboard codes are stored in consecutive half-words of memory. Eight routines are supplied: BINDEC 1 through BINDEC 9 with the exception of BINDEC 6.
Source Language: LAP6
Catalog: November 1969
