ATEPO: Auto Test in Elementary Programming and Operation
5,8-15
of a PDP-5/8 Computer

Submitted by: Rutgers University, Electrical Engineering
Department, New Brunswick, New Jersey

The program will type questions or instructions to be performed
by the operator of a 4K PDP-5/8. The program will check
to see if the operator has answered the questions correctly.
If this is the case, it will type the next question or instruction.

Catalog: November 1969
