LAP6-1C (Modification to LAP6)
L-30
Author: Unknown
This is a modification procedure for modifying LAP6 to adapt it to:
  1. LINC-8 Teletype and mnemonics SKP, PDP, TYP and EXC.
  2. Fanfold paper in the Teletype.
The Teletype, mnemonic, and fanfold mods are independent of one another.
Catalog: November 1969
