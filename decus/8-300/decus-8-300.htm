Noise Generator
8-300

H. -D. Schenk, Deutsche Forschungs - und Versuchsanstalt fur
Luft - und Raumfahrt EV, Flughafen, Germany

This program, which is written as a subroutine, creates a
pseudo-random voltage with a gaussian probability density
function. Also the appropriate binary noise is available. The
bandwidth can be selected by the programmer.

Minimum Hardware:
4K PDP-8, D/A Converter (#55),
Digital Output Register (#30)
Storage Requirement:
One page of memory
Source Language:
MACRO-8

Catalog: July 1973
