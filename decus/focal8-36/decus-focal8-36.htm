Determinot Program
FOCAL8-36

Ron Dorman, Georgia Institute of Technology, Nuclear
Research Center, Atlanta, Georgia

The Determinot program is a simple program which may be
used to find the determinant of a square matrix of dimension
2 x 2 to 6 x 6. The method used in finding the determinant
is based on the definition of the determinant and involves an
N summation of products of N matrix terms with the proper
inversion sign.

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8, ASR33
Source Language:
