FPP-12/FOCAL-12 Reduction of Auto Analyzer Data for Pharmaceuticals
12-116
Author: L. L. Alber, M. W. Overton and Dr. D. E. Smith, U. 5. Food and Drug Administration, Chicago, Illinois
An 8-point least-squares fitting algorithm for the FPP-12 is used as a subroutine of FOCAL-12 to reduce Auto Analyzer data previously stored on LINCtape by ADTAPE/ADCON operating on-line with up to 16 instruments.
Minimum Hardware: 8K PDP-12, 2 LINCtape units, DF32 disk, FPP-12
Other Programs Needed: FPPASM Assembler
Source Language: LAP6-DIAL and FPPASM
Catalog: July 1974
