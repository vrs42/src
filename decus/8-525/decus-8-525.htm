DAFFT/PAFFT are overlays to DAQUAN (EAE) which support
signal averaging in the time domain (up to 1024 double
precision points), and FFT into frequency domain giving up to
1024 real and 1024 complex coefficients.

Minimum Hardware:
LAB 8/E (Advanced) with EAE
Source Language:
PAL-8

Catalog: July 1973
