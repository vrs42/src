MERGE
8-481a

Geoffrey Chase, Portsmouth Abbey School, Portsmouth,
Rhode Island

MERGE reads any number of binary paper tapes
and combines their contents into a single binary
output tape with a single checksum. Field pseudoops
are correctly copied; ASCII diagnostics enclosed
between rubouts are ignored.

Minimum Hardware:
4K PDP-8, ASR33 (HSR/P
optional)
Source Language:
PAL III

Catalog:July 1973
