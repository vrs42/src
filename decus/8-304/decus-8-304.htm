Pseudo-Noise (P-N) Sequence Test
8-304

R. G. Smith, Carleton University, Ottawa, Ontario, Canada

This program can be used to determine the statistical charac-
teristics of a pseudo-random sequence generator. The probabi-
lity density function can be studied with the aid of an am-
plitude histogram, and measures of the "randomness" can be
determined with correlation diagrams, sample function dis-
plays, and scattergrams. All diagrams are displayed on the
CRT.

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8 with EAE, A/D Converter,
CRT display
Storage Requirement:
Program: 0-776; Data: 2001-5776;
Axis points: 1001-2000
