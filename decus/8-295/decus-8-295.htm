COMBIN
8-295

A. Moses, Computer Application Engineering Company,
El Paso, Texas

COMBIN is a combined loader for BIN and CBL paper tapes.
The CBL format tapes described in DECUS NO. 8-26 can be
loaded in about 75% of the time required to load equivalent
BIN tapes. Therefore, many installations now have most of
their tapes converted into CBL format. The problem was to
have both a BIN and a CBL loader on the last page of memory
so that they could be protected. As a sacrifice, the RIM
loader is no longer on the protected last page of memory.

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8/L, 4K
Storage Requirement:
7600-7772
Restrictions:
No High Speed Reader
Source Language:
PAL
