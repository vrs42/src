PAL-D Patch
8-216

Edward A. Taft III, Saint Mark's School, Southborough,
Massachusetts

PAL-D Patch is designed to provide the following added
features that are present in field one. It will store excess
symbols in field one; ignore rubout characters in input from
the high speed reader; and provide further formatting of 3rd pass
output onto 11-inch long pages. Two known bugs in PAL-D
are also corrected.

Minimum Hardware:
8K PDP-8

Other Programs Needed:
PAL-D (DEC-D8-ASAA-PB)

Source Language:
PAL-D

Catalog:July 1973
