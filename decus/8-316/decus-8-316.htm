CORR (Compute Correlation Matrix)
8-316

J. N. R. Jeffers
Submitted by: A. J. P. Gore, The Nature Conservancy,
Lancashire, England

This program computes the means and standard deviations and
the matrix of correlation of coefficients, for multivariate data.
A separate version of the program transforms the data to their
common logarithms before computing means, sums of squares,
etc.

Minimum Hardware:
4K PDP-8/I, DF32 Disk, High
Speed Reader
Other Programs Needed:
FORTRAN D Disk Systems Programs
Source Language:
FORTRAN D

Catalog:July 1973
