Octal Debugging Technique with View
8-349

Larry McGimsey, Western Kentucky University, Bowling
Green, Kentucky

This program permits the display of 208 consecutive core
locations on a LAB-8 system from field 0 or field l. The
program is a modification of ODT that uses 4 pages instead of
3, and allows operations on both field 0 and field l. The
word search and punch routines have been deleted.

Minimum Hardware:
4K PDP-8, ASR33, AX08 with
scope
Storage Requirement:
10008 locations, plus 4 and 5 in
field 0 and 4, 5 and 6 in field l
Source Language:
PAL-D

Catalog:July 1973
