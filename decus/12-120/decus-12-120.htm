DUAL Assembler
12-120
Author: John R. Raines, Northwestern University Medical School, Chicago, Illinois
DUAL is derived from QUIP, DECUS NO. 12-54. It has been given extra pseudo-ops, etc. to make it nearly DIAL compatible. In addition, it offers literals; address multiplication, division and rotation; several fold increase in assembly speed; a second set of conditional assembly pseudoops and some additional pseudo-ops. Updated August 1973.
Minimum Hardware: PDP-12 with DIAL-MS and EAE, TTY, preferably ASR35
Source Language: DUAL
Catalog: July 1974
