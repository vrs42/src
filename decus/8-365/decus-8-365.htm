CARD
8-365

Herbert Poppe, Lamont Geological Observatory of
Columbia University, Palisades, New York

Reads cards from a card reader and transfers the characters to
DECtape as an ASCII file compatible with Disk/DECtape
Monitor System. The program runs with the interrupt enabled.
CARD, in effect, allows card input to the PAL-D Assembler,
for example.

Minimum Hardware:
4K PDP-8, 1 DECtape Drive and
Control, Card Reader (CR01, 2,
3 or CR8I/L) card Control
Other Programs Needed:
Disk/DECtape Monitor System
(DEC-D8-SDAB)
Storage Requirement:
Program: 0000-1577; Buffers:
2000-3001, 4000-5405
Restrictions:
Ignores card columns 61-80
Source Language:
PAL-D

Catalog:July 1973
