Michaelis-Menten Kinetics
FOCAL8-198

Stan Vivian, University of Manitoba, Faculty of Medicine,
Winnipeg, Canada

This is a FOCAL program to provide maximum likelihood esti-
mates of the parameters VMAX and K of the Michaelis-
Menten equation. Standard errors and both 95 and 99%
confidence limits of the parameters are also provided. Fitted
data points and the reciprocals of the estimates are printed
out for graphical purposes.

Minimum Hardware:
8K PDP-8, TTY
Storage Requirement:
FOCAL Text: Locations 100-4705
Field 1
Restrictions:
Maximum of 30 data points
Source Language:
FOCAL '69

Catalog: July 1973
