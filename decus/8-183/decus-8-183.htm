The WANG Loader
8-183

L. C. Wang
Submitted by: Richard E. Hummer, University of Maryland,
College Park, Maryland

The WANG Loader will load any program that ends at
location 7777. The program consists of 8 instructions that
are loaded via the toggle switches, and a tape that will
boot-in the BIN and RIM loaders.

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8 with ASR-33

Catalog: November 1969
