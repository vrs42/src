Peter Andrews and Charles Wagner, Fairchild R&D,
Palo Alto, California

A one-page program which allows insertion of instruction
(xxxx) in location (nnnn) by means of the TTY keyboard.
A feature of automatically incrementing the current address
permits rapid insertion of blocks of data or instructions.
Typing "RUB-OUT" reinitializes the program.

Storage Requirement:
1758 locations (1 page)

Catalog:November 1969
