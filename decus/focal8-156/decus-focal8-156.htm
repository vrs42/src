Blackjack for FOCAL
FOCAL8-156

Vincent Perriello, Toft School, Watertown, Connecticut

This program was written to emphasize the versatility of the
PDP-8 FOCAL while serving as on amusement to new or in-
experienced users. It occupies nearly all of the buffer space,
with the subscripted "card" variables.

Minimum Hardware:
4K PDP-8
Other Programs Needed:
FOCAL 5/69 (DECUS NO.
FOCAL8-52)
Source Language:
FOCAL 5/69

Catalog: July 1973
