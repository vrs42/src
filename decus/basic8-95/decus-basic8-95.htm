MADMAZ Maze Generator
BASIC8-95
Author: Christopher A. Kryzan, Northwestern University, Chicago, IL
Operating System: EDU-30
Source Language: BASIC
Abstract: Computers have many non-scientific applications in addition to their technical side, one of which is found in demonstrations and gaming. An interesting sub-genre of this is the construction and solution of puzzles. MADMAZ is designed to create 15 X 15 maze puzzles, replete with solutions as well. Execution can be quite lengthy, but the results are well worth the wait.

Catalog: August 1978
