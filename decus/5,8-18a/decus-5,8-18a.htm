Binary Tape Disassembiy Program
5,8-18A

G. A. Sabin, NRL-USRD, Orlando; Florida

Disassembles a PDP-5 or 8 program, which is on tape in
BIN format. It prints the margin setting, address, octal
contents, mnemonic interpretation (PAL) of the octal
contents. A normal program or a program which uses Floating
Point may be disassembled.

Catalog:November 1969
