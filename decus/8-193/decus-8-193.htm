DISP
8-193

S. G. Wellcome, Trinity College, Hartford, Connecticut

DISP provides a simple means of using the 34D Display with
FORTRAN-D. It allows the operator to display varying
numbers of points with movable X and Y axes.

Minimum Hardware:
4K PDP-8 with DF32 Disk
Other Programs Needed:
FORTRAN-D Compiler
(DEC-08-AFCO)

Source Language:
PAL-D

Storage Requirement:
600-7778, 7400-75778

Restrictions:
Destroys FORTRAN-D disk
read/write option (e. g. Read
3, 10)

Catalog:November 1969
