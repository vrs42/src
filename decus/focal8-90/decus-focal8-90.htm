X-Y Plotter Patch for FOCAL '69
FOCAL8-90

Roy H. Swatzell, Jr., University of Alabama, School of
Medicine, Birmingham, Alabama

This routine is designed to give the user point or line plotting
capability through the FOCAL Interpreter.

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8, ASR33, X-Y Plotter
(Digital)
Other Programs Needed:
FOCAL-69, BIN Loader
Storage Requirement:
1118 locations (in FOCAL)
Restrictions:
This routine replaces the arctangent
and log routines in FOCAL
Source Language:
PAL III

Catalog:July 1973
