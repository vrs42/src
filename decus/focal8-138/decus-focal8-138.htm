WCXT: The Wilcoxon Matched-Pairs Signed-Ranks Test for
FOCAL8-138
Non Parametric Data

G. C. Ongley, Graylingwell Hospital, Chichester, Sussex,
England

A "T" test for non parametric data. It compares differences
between two samples of paired data for magnitude and direction,
large differences being given more weight than small
differences.
