POSTERBASIC8-73
Submitted by: Bradford Huntress
Operating System: EDUCOMP EDU250 under OS/8
Source Language: EDUCOMP EDU250 BASIC
Special Hardware Required: PDP-8/E with 3 teletypes and single DECtape unit
Abstract: This program, an adaptation of BANNER, was designed to run under EDUCOMP EDU250 BASIC, but can easily be adapted to other BASICs as well. All letters now have rounded comers where appropriate, and no two characters, except the letter O and the number zero, are the same.
Media Price Code: D2, G5
Catalog: August 1978
