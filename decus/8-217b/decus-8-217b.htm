PALM
8-217B

Mathew Simon, Computer Applications, Inc., New York,
New York

PALM is a bank 0 modification to PAL-D whose purpose is to
direct output that usually goes to an ASR-33/35 to PALR
(DECUS NO. 8-217A).

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8 with two banks of core
storage, DF-32 Disk, TU-20
magnetic tape drive

Other Programs Needed:
PAL-D

Catalog: July 1973
