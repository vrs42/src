C528: Paper Tape Conversion 5 Track (SIRIUS) to 8 Track
8-282
(A.S.C.I.I.)

A. J. P. Gore, The Nature Conservancy, Merlewood
Research Station, Grange-over-Sands, Lancashire, England

5 track paper tape is translated into 8 track pclper tape by
inverting the 5 track tape to make the 3 tracks and sprocket
holes coincide with the corresponding facilities of the 8
track tape readers of the PDP-8.

Minimum Hardware:
4K PDP-8/I, DF32 Disk, HSR/P
Other Programs Needed:
Disk systems programs
Source Language:
PAL-D

Catalog:July 1973
