Restoring Symbolprint
8-671

A. Moses, Applied Math Co. , Anthony, Texas

RESTORING SYMBOLPRINT automatically reloads the orig-
inal contents of locations 10, 11 and 12 which have been
destroyed by using Symbolprint after compiling a program in
4K FORTRAN.

Other Programs Needed:
4K FORTRAN Compiler
(DEC-08-AFCI)
Storage Requirement:
4K
Source Language:
PAL III
