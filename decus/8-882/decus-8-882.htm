Bomb Drop Version: February 1978
8-882
Author: Bruce A Brigham
Glastonbury, CT
Operating System: Paper Tape
Source Language: PAL-III
Memory Required: 2K
Special Hardware Required: VT50

Abstract: Bomb Drop must be played on VT50 (or VT52 with alterations) running at 1200 baud or higher.
Game generates a ground pattern with random positions and random number of tanks (number of tanks
increases as you move North). You must kill all tanks before bombs are used up. You are armded with 2000
pounds of bombs and five (5) lasers (lasers are shot straight down from aircraft - 5 total). Tybe "B" to drop
bombs, type "L" to shoot laser. Win one battle, move 10 miles South. Red City is 70 miles North of start
point (DMZ). The object is to win at Red City.
Only the best win the war!

Media Price Code: D4, F5
Catalog: June 1979
