Perpetual Calendar
8-71

E. Singer, McGill University, Montreal, Quebec, Canada

The program is designed as a computer demonstration. When
a valid date is fed into the computer, the corresponding day
of the week is typed out. The program is based on the
Gregorian Calendar and is limited to years between 1500
and 4095.

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8 with an ASR-33 Teletype

Storage Requirement:
20-1333

Catalog:November 1969
