BLOOPD - Blood Pressure Display Program
12-41
Author: Julia A. Voland
Submitted by: Dr. Nelson E. Leatherman, Indiana University, Bloomington, Indiana
BLOOPD is primarily for visual information only. It displays either the blood pressure waveform on a calibrated scope, or the digitized values of four parameters of the blood pressure. A printout of the values is also provided. All options are selected by teletype.
Minimum Hardware: PDP-12A
Storage Requirement: Two fields, total 2713(8) locations
Source Language: LAP6
Catalog: July 1974
