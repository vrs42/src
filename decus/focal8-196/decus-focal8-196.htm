Fisher's Exact Test
FOCAL8-196

Robert M. Smith, University of Alabama Medical Center,
Birmingham, Alabama

This non-parametric technigue is used with 2 X 2 bivariate
tables when cell freguencies are insufficient for Chi-square
tests. The program prints a table with labels and marginal
frequencies and an exact probability of occurrence of the
freguency distribution.

Minimum Hardware:
4K PDP-8/I, ASR/KSR33
Source Language:
FOCAL

Catalog: July 1973
