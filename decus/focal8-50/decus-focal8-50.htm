FOCAL Version of RC Active Filter
FOCAL8-50

Bean and Roman, University of Texas, Southwestern Medical
School, Dallas, Texas

This program is a FOCAL version of a program by Kincaid
and Shirley as published in Electronic Design Volume 13.
Derived from two fundamental equations, it can be used to
design Butterworth or Chebyshev filters in either low-pass or
high-pass versions of each.

Minimum Hardware:
4K PDP-8
Source Language:
FOCAL-69

Catalog: July 1973
