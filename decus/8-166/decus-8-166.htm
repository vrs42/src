The PDPMAP Assembly System
8-166

Thomas H. Johnson and Michael S. Wolfberg, Moore School
of Electrical Engineering, University of Pennsylvania,
Philadelphia, Pennsylvania

This report describes the PDPMAP Assembly System which is
used to assemble symbolic programs written for a PDP-8 or
DEC-338 with up to 16K memory locations. The system is
implemented at the University of Pennsylvania on an IBM
7040 and DEC PDP-8 connected by a high-speed data channel
(IBM 7904 and DEC DM03). The PDPMAP System uses the
powerful assembler of a large computer (IBM 7040 MAP
Assembler) to quickly assemble programs for a small computer.

Report only available.
