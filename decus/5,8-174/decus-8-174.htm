Medium
8-174

Lance A. Carnes
Submitted by: James A. Gillespie, Lawrence Radiation
Laboratory, Berkeley, California

Medium is a demonstration program for use on the PDP-5 or
PDP-8 family. Messages typed on the Teletype are displayed
on the scope, advancing across the screen from right to left
similar to the Times Square News Sign.

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-5 or PDP-8 family with
34D or VC8/I Scope.

Source Language:
LRL PDP-5 Assembly Language
(DECUS No. 5-13)

Storage Requirement:
418-15008

Catalog:November 1969
