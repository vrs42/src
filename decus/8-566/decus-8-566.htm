Andres T. Siy, Capitol Institute of Technology, Kensington,
Maryland

Program to evaluate the partial fraction expansion of a rational
function N(s)/D(s), that has real coefficients and D(s) are
written in linear or quadratic factors. Samples of control
system reduction to its "Foster Form" canonical form, and
network synthesis are given,

Minimum Hardware:
8K PDP-8/I, ASR33, HSR/P
Other Programs Needed:
8K FORTRAN Compiler, 8K Assembler,
8K Linking Loader, User's Program
Source Language:
8K FORTRAN

Catalog:July 1973
