Revised Octal Memory Dump
8-487

Masashi Kamii
Submitted by: Tomoji Yanagita, The Central Institute of
Experimental Animals, Nogawa Kawasaki, Japan

This routine will output on the teletype an absolute address
plus octal contents of 8 memory locations per one line and a
blank line at every 8 lines. Except for the first address the
leftmost address is always a multiple of 8, making it easy to
search addresses.

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8, ASR33
Source Language:
PAL III

Catalog:July 1973
