"HORSERACE"
FOCAL8-101

William Garcia, Jr., Dow Badische, Freeport, Texas

Simple, but a very exciting horserace, based on the frequency
of numbers generated. Ten decimal numbers, 0-9, are used
for a better distribution. The highest frequency at which a
number appears is paired with that of the lowest frequency.

Minimum Hardware:
4K PDP-8, ASR33
Source Language:
FOCAL 1968
