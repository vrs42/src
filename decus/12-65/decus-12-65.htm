PISH- Poststimulus Time and Interspike - Interval Histogram
12-65
Author: Dr. D. J. Woodward, University of Rochester
Submitted by: Ron Carter, Digital Equipment Corporation, Maynard, Massachusetts
PISH consists of two programs, ISH (interspike interval histogram) and PSTH (poststimulus time histogram). ISH and PSTH compute density histograms of the frequency of time intervals between events or of frequency of events after synchronizing or stimulating pulses. The programs are capable of analyzing times of occurrence of single events and of groups of events defined as bursts.
Minimum Hardware: 8K PDP-12, KW12A, LINCtapes
Source Language: DIAL
Catalog: July 1974
