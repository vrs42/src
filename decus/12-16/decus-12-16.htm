MODCLK
12-16
Author: Stephen J. Mayor, Medical College of Ohio at Toledo, Toledo, Ohio
This program is used as a test of the KW-12 clock which uses the sense switches and Teletype. Each sense switch corresponds to a particular clock frequency and depressing a switch causes the Teletype bell to ring at a certain rate as follows: SNS0, 100Hz, 1/SEC. SNS1, 1KHZ, 1/2SEC; SNS2, 10KHZ, 1/4 SEC; SNS3, 100 KHZ, 1/6 SEC; SNS4, 400 KHZ, 1/8 SEC.
Minimum Hardware: 4K PDP-12A and KW-12 clock
Storage Requirement: 242 Octal locations
Source Language: LAP6-DIAL
Catalog: July 1974
