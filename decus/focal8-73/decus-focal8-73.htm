Real Matrix Inversion
FOCAL8-73

Harold Metcalf, SUNY Stony Brook, Stony Brook, New York

Inverts a real, square matrix using FOCAL. This is essentially
a translation of the IBM MINV.

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8
Restrictions:
6 x 6 matrix is the largest that
can be done with 4K
Source Language:
FOCAL

Catalog: July 1973
