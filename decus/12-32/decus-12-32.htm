COMPAR12
12-32
Author: D. F. Pavlock and S. G. Wellcome, Digital Equipment Corporation, Maynard, Massachusetts
COMPAR12 allows the user to compare either source or binary DIAL files by name, or specified blocks of tape or disk by absolute block numbers. Any discrepancies are displayed on the scope. With 8K, the comparison is done 10 blocks at a time. If 12K is available, it is done 20 blocks at a time.
Other Programs Needed: DIAL-MS I/O routines
Storage Requirement: 8K
Source Language: LAP6-DIAL
Catalog: July 1974
