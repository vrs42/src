TEACH
FOCAL8-103

Edward Steinfeld, Digital Equipment Corporation,
Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania

This is an example of what could be accomplished in the
computer aided instruction realm. TEACH is only a sample
and does not carry the student beyond the first hour of in-
struction. The program is divided into three sections: First,
the instruction segment; Second, six problems with answers
but no explanation; the third section is comprised of an
explanation and six problems, with the option to continue or
stop.

Minimum Hardware:
4K PDP-8
Restrictions:
No functions
Source Language:
FOCAL-69

Catalog: July 1973
