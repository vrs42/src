FOCAL-8 Patch for LINC-8 Display
FOCAL8-6

Peter Goldstern, Digital Equipment Corporation

This patch causes FDXS and FDYS commands in FOCAL to be
displayed on the LINC-8 Display. This patch applies only to
DEC-08-AJAD-PB, FOCAL.

Minimum Hardware:
LINC-8
Other Programs Needed:
FOCAL DEC-08-AJAD-PB

Catalog: July 1973
