GASSERBASIC8-49
Submitted by: Kent Springer
Operating System: EDU-20/25
Source Language: BASIC
Abstract: This program solves problems involving the Ideal Gas Law equation for any of the four variables in the equation. It will accept temperature in degrees Farenheit, Centigrade, or Kelvin, pressure in P.S.I., atmospheres, or mm of mercury, and volume in liters or milliliters.
