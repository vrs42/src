Using the High Speed Punch with FOCAL
FOCAL8-80

Harold Metcalf, Physics Department, SUNY Stony Brook,
Stony Brook, New York

This program enables the use of the high speed punch in
FOCAL by direct or indirect command, in much the same way
that the asterisk enables the optical reader.

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8, high speed punch
Other Programs Needed:
FOCAL - 69
Source Language:
PAL III

Catalog:July 1973
