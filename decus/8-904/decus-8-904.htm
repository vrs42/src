Line Printer Patch to use with FOCAL Version: June 1978
8-904
Author: T. Jeevanandam and S. S. Lamba
Indian Institute of Technology, India
Operating System: Independent
Source Language: PAL-8/PAL-III
Memory Required: 8K
Other Software Required: FOCL.S (8K version) DECUS No. FOCAL8-148*
Special Hardware Required: Line Printer LE08 and Teletype interfaced with PDP-8E

Abstract: An easy way of selecting the output device, either a teletype of a line printer, is illustrated. The
patch is an overlay which can be loaded after loading the FOCL.S (DECUS No. FOCAL8-148*) compiler.
The routines occupy the core area allocated for user-coded subroutines.

Note: * DECUS No. FOCAL8-148 has been obsoleted from the DECUS library, however, DECUS No.
FOCAL8-227 replaces it in part.
