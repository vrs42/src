Four New Functions for FOCAL 5/69
FOCAL8-164

Vincent E. Perriello, Taft School, Watertown, Connecticut

This patch to FOCAL 5/69 (DECUS NO. FOCAL8-52) enables
four new functions that make FOCAL more user-oriented than
ever. With the aid of these additions the user can tailor
FOCAL to his own needs. They give the ability to write or
call subroutines in core, read the switch register and read from
any core location, an aid in debugging. This patch wipes out
FADC, FDIS, FDXS and FNEW.

Other Programs Needed:
FOCAL 5/69 (DECUS NO.
FOCAL8-52)
