Program to Mate PAL III With Symbolic Editor
8-515

G. Chase, Portsmouth Abbey School, Portsmouth, Rhode Island
This program enables PAL III (in field 0 or 1) to read Symbolic
Editor's text buffer directly (field 1 or 0) without paper tape
input to PAL. Editor and its storage areas are unmodified.
PAL loses its low speed input routine but retains all other
options.

Minimum Hardware:
8K PDP-8, TTY
Other Programs Needed:
PAL III; Symbolic Editor
Source Language:
PAL III

Catalog: July 1973
