Perpetual Calendar (BASIC Version)
8-463

Daniel Gutierrez, Granada Hills High School, Granada Hills,
California

This program is similar to DECUS NO. 8-71 but is written in
BASIC. It will provide the day of the week for any date
entered. It is particularly useful for demonstrating the com-
puter's ability to perform simple problems as well as more
complex ones.

Minimum Hardware:
4K PDP-8 with Teletype
Source language: BASIC

Catalog:February 1972
