decus-8-102a comes with a HELP-style boot loader.  Here's what to
toggle (HSR version):

0020 LA
6011 DEP
5020 DEP
6016 DEP
7012 DEP
7010 DEP
7440 DEP
2027 DEP
3002 DEP
5020 DEP
0022 LA
     START

With the tape positioned on the first segment, this should load and start
a version of the BIN loader to load the second segment.

Alternatively, if your BIN loader fits in the last half of the last page,
you can just use that to load the second segment.

The starting address for LISP itself is 3000 to re-initialize, or 3001 to
retain existing objects.
