TENNIS
12-177
Author: Lewis Brenner, Philadelphia Police Department Chemical Laboratory, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania
The game of tennis Is played on the PDP-12 computer using the VR14 display scope. The rackets are controlled by A/D knobs 0 and 4 (left paddle) and 3 and 7 (right paddle). SSW0, when set, generates a ball from the center net. SSW5, when set, returns to the DIAL monitor on tape unit 0. The teletype bell rings when a player scores.
Minimum Hardware: PDP-12, VR-14 display scope
Other Programs Needed: DIAL System
Storage Requirement: Less than 2K
Source Language: DIAL
Catalog: December 1974
