Fields
8-191

D. Whiteley, International Computers limited, Kidsgrove,
Stoke-on-Trent, England

Fields, a demonstration program, calculates and displays
the surface potential of a given boundary conditional plane.
Each output facility is called by a 338 Display pushbutton
giving a numerical and/or pictorial result.

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8 with High-Speed punch
ASR-33 Teletype and 338
Display

Storage Requirement:
8K

Catalog: November 1969
