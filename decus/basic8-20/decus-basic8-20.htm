Games-SET 2
BASIC8-20
Abstract: This package contains:

BASEBALL GAME SIMULATION-Dave Davidson and Bill Weaver, Simulates a baseball game, keeping track of runners, innings, scores, etc.

HORSE RACE GAME-Tom Adametz, Simulates a race between 5 horses. Initial stake is $500.

PDP-8 500 AUTO RACE GAME-Tom Adametz, Simulates an auto race.

KENO-V. Fazio, Plays the game of KENO on the computer.

YATZEE-Bruce Baker III, Simulates the dice game YATZEE on the computer.

SIMULATION BASEBALL-Jeff Moscow; Revision by Bob Tedford, Simultes a baseball game. Uses OS/8 BASIC.

FOOTBALL-Ed Vogel, Plays football with the computer. Allows for both offense and defense.

HORSE RACING-Richard Balekdjian, Race between 7 horses; initial stake $750.
