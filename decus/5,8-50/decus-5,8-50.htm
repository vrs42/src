Additions to Symbolic Tape Format Generator (DEC-8-21-4)
5,8-50

Richard Merrill, Digital Equipment Corporation, Maynard,
Massachusetts

These routines allow the user to perform further useful
functions by the addition of a few octal patches. By making
the appropriate octal patches via the toggles, the Format
Generator can also format FORTRAN tapes, shorten tape by
converting space to tabs, and convert the type of tape.

Catalog: November 1969
