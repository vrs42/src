Liquid Scintillation Counting: Conversion of CPM to DPM in
12-53
Author: Double-label Experiments
Stephen J. Mayor, Ph. D, Medical College of Ohio at Toledo, Toledo, Ohio This program takes the raw data outputted from the LSC's (Packard Model 3380) teletype punch, and using the Okitz equations, calculates the DPM for two isotopes of each sample. The AES ratio is used to calculate percentage of efficiency and spillover for each isotope.
Minimum Hardware: PDP-12A, Teletype punch and reader
Storage Requirement: 4096 words
Source Language: FOCAL-4K
Catalog: July 1974
