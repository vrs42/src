Interrupt Duplicator for Binary Object Tapes
8-502

G. Chase, Portsmouth Abbey School, Portsmouth, Rhode Island

The program copies absolute binary tapes (produced by PAL,
MACRO, ODT, etc.) from any of three readers onto any one
of three punches.

This routine will not copy SABR or other relocatable binaries.

Minimum Hardware:
4K PDP-8, one or more on-line
TTYs; HSR/P optional
Source Language:
PAL III

Catalog:July 1973
