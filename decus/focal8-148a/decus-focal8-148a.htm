FOCL.S, An Expanded Language for Small Computers, Based
FOCAL8-148A
on FOCAL

D. E. Wrege, Georgia Institute of Technology, Atlanta, Georgia
Submitted by: John Alderman

This dialect of FOCAL-69 is intended to be a more powerful
superset of FOCAL-69 and is syntactically compatible with it.
Additional features include: multiple subscripted arrays;
line continuation character; conditional "DO;" computed
line numbers; expanded access to internal and external
processor environment.

Minimum Hardware:
4K PDP-8 with ASR33
Source Language:
PAL-8

Catalog:July 1973
