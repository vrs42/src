FARRAY, A FOCAL FNEW for Two Dimensional Arrays in 8K

FOCAL

Hans Mees and Floor Anthoni, Medical Biological Laboratory, T.
N.O., Rijswijk, The Netherlands

FARRAY uses Field 1 from top to bottom (competitively with
the text area) for the storage of one - or two - dimensional
arrays, Arrays can be defined in integer or 3- or 4- word
floating point format. Arrays can be created or deleted
dynamically during program execution,

8K PDP-8

overlay
