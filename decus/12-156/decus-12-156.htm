MUSIC12
12-156
Author: Philip H. Jensen
Submitted by: James C. Good, Jamesville-DeWitt Central Schools, DeWitt, New York
MUSIC12 is a program for the PDP-12 user which allows use of the teletype keyboard as a piano keyboard. There are 4 Octaves of notes, including sharps and flats. The music can be played directly from the keyboard, or from previously punched paper tape via the teletype paper tape reader.
Other Programs Needed: Monitor Command
Source Language: LAP6-DIAL
Catalog: July 1974
