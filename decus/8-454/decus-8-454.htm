Radio Teletype to ASCII
8-454

Carl Kishline, University of Wisconsin, Parkside Instructional
Computing Center, Kenosha, Wisconsin

This program reads 5-channel tape as generated by a model 15
or 19 teletype and prints (and optionally punches) the corre-
sponding characters in ASCII code. It thus allows computer
operators to enjoy the beautiful art work which amateur radio
operators produce.

Minimum Hardware:
4K PDP-8, ASR33
Storage Requirement:
2 pages
Source Language:
PAL-D

Catalog:February 1972
