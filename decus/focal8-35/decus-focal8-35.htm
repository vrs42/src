Rootfinder Program
FOCAL8-35

Ron Dorman, Georgia Institute of Technology, Nuclear

Research Center, Atlanta, Georgia

The Rootfinder program is a simple procedure, for use in
determining the real roots of any suitable function. The
program uses a conventional search to find root-containing
intervals followed by a binary search (successive approximation
method) to converge on the root value.
