Analysis of Variance Randomized Block "F" Test
FOCAL8-70

C. T. Lund, Canada Department of Agriculture, Vineland
Station, Ontario, Canada

The purpose of this program is to isolate variation in an ex-
periment attributable to treatments and replicates, and test
this variation for significance.

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8/L, ASR33
Restrictions:
The block design must be complete
Source Language:
FOCAL 1968
