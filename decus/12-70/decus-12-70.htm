COMPLT
12-70
Author: Harry Bryant, Krusen Research: Center, Temple University, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania
COMPLT is a SABRised version of the DECUS NO. 8-168 plotting package. It retains all features of that program, with the additional advantage of allowing it to be called as a FORTRAN subroutine. A DECtape version is available for PDP-8 users.
Minimum Hardware: PDP-12A
Source Language: PS-8 SABR
Catalog: July 1974
