SPASTIC - A System for Programming Angles, Scaler and
FOCAL8-224
Timer, by Internal Counting

C. Richard Desper, Army Materials and Mechanics Research
Center, Watertown, Massachusetts

The FOCAL interpreter has been modified to control a simple
PDP-8/L interface for X-ray diffraction experiments. Control
operations are accessed through a set of FOCAL functions
which control four stepping motors, an internal data break
scaler, a timer based on crystal clock interrupt, and the X-
ray shutter solenoid.
