Center of Gravity Calculations
FOCAL8-222

Joel D, Scheraga, Stamford High School, Stamford,
Co:-inecticut

This program, written especially for students of Model
Rocketry, enables the user to determine the center of gravity
of the rocket and the weight of the rocket 1. minus the
engine; 2. including the engine; 3. at the time of burnout.

Minimum Hardware:
4K PDP-8, TTY
Other Programs Needed:
DECUS NO. FOCAL8-52a
Source Language:
FOCAL 5/69

Catalog: July 1973
