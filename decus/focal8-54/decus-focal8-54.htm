Channel Information and Inverted Histogram Plot
FOCAL8-54

Thomas J. Ford, White Mountains Regional High School,
Whitefield, New Hampshire

This program will accept up to 36 channels of information
stored as A(I) during 'T' passes. It will then plot an inverted
histogram using the symbol '[ ],' spacing through vacant
channels, and subtracting one count till all channels are
vacant.

Source Language:
FOCAL-69
