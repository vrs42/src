SCED: Scope Editor for the AX08
8-255

R. H. S. Carpenter, Physiology Laboratory, University of
Cambridge, England

This scope editor for the LAB-8, offers fast and convenient
editing of symbolic text. Lines may be selected by reference
to their initial character, and a movable marker may be used
to select particular portions of a selected line.

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8, Ax,08, High Speed Punch
and Reader
Other Programs Needed:
AX08 Symbol Generator (DECUS
NO. 8-158)
Source Language:
PAL III

Catalog: July 1973
