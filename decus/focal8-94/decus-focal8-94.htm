Multidimensional Integration by Gaussian Quadrature
FOCAL8-94

H. Bradford Thompson, University of Toledo, Department of
Chemistry, Toledo, Ohio

A subprogram provides multidimensional integration of a known
function by Gaussian quadrature. The user may define the
function, integration limits, and number of points used.
Gaussian quadrature is valuable within FOCAL because of its
low error for a limited number of calculated points.

Minimum Hardware:
4K PDP-8
Source Language:
