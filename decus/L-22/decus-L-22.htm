A New PDP-8 Display Instruction for LINC-8
L-22
Author: R. W. Tuttle, K. Pavel, and C. F. Stroebel, Institute of Living Hospital, Hartford, Connecticut
This documentation describes hardware changes for a new PDP-8 IOT instruction so that the LINC-8 display unit may be controlled directly by the PDP-8.
Catalog: November 1969
