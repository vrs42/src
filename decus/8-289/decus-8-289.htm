"ULKA" The Ultimate Kaleidoscope
8-289

Dr. A. S. French, University of Alberta, Edmonton, Alberta,
Canada

"ULKA" is a true kaleidoscope program for use on the LAB-8
computer. Unlike other kaleidoscope programs "ULKA" is
completely automatic. No user interaction is required to
produce a dazzling array of patterns.

Minimum Hardware:
LAB-8
Source Language:
PAL-D

Catalog: July 1973
