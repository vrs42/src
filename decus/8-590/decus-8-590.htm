Matrix Inversion
8-590

Jean-Francois Echallier and Jacques Pernier, I.N.S.E.R.M.,
Bron, France

This subroutine inverts a matrix of real numbers residing in
core in floating format (three words). The Gauss-Jordan
method of elimination is used to perform the inversion.

Calling program, routine and data are in the same field.

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8/I with EAE
Other Programs Needed:
Floating Point Package
(DEC-08-YQYA-PB)
Source Language:
PAL

Catalog:July 1973
