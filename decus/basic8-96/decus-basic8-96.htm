Paper Tape Message Generator
BASIC8-96
Author: Christopher A. Kryzan, Northwestern University, Chicago, IL
Operating System: EDU-30
Source Language: BASIC
Abstract: A variety of programs to produce punched tape messages have been published, but this particular version proves to be one of the most efficient yet designed in BASIC. The program consists simply of a data list of characters and a routine to enter and output the message, creating punched tape records of up to 400 characters in length.
