PRECIS, A Program to Scan a Binary Tape
8-584

David Rosenthal, Research Triangle Institute, Research
Triangle Park, North Carolina

This program reads a 4K binary tape and prints out all starting
and final addresses of blocks, number of blocks and checksum
indication.

The program includes a stand-alone subroutine for printing
octal numbers.

Minimum Hardware:
4K PDP-8, ASR33, HSR
Source Language:
PAL III

Catalog: July 1973
