Charge Account
FOCAL8-122

Frederick W. Holzwarth, George Washington High School,
Philadelphia, Pennsylvania

This program is useful in teaching high school students manip-
ulation of subscripted arrays. It also gives academic students
an introduction to business application. The data included
was taken from one of the types of charge accounts offered by
a local department store.

Minimum Hardware:
4K PDP-8/S, ASR33
Source Language:
FOCAL-69

Catalog:July 1973
