RAND (Computation of Random Fractions)
8-319

J. N. R. Jeffers
Submitted by: A. J. P. Gore, The Nature Conservancy,
Lancashire, England

This program computes a designated number of random frac-
tions in the range 0.0000 to 0.9999. The computed fractions
represent a sample from a uniform distribution.

Minimum Hardware:
4K PDP-8/I, DF32 Disk, High
Speed Reader
Other Programs Needed:
FORTRAN D Disk Systems Programs
Source Language:
FORTRAN D

Catalog:July 1973
