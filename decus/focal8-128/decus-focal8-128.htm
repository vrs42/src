Probability (2P); From t ("Student") Distribution
FOCAL8-128

Milton Landowne, M. D., U.S. Army Institute of
Environmental Medicine, Natick, Massachusetts

Calculates probability that a difference between means is due
to chance, when given the number of degrees of freedom and
the ratio (t) of the difference between means and the standard
error of this difference.
