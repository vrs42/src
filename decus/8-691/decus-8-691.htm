ACCK Timeshare Accounting System
8-691

Lynn H. Macey, Computer Services, Associated Colleges of
Central Kansas, McPherson, Kansas

The ACCK Timeshare Accounting System is a direct replace-
ment for the present method of running CAT under the system
account. Historical data is kept on DECtape and various
reports may be generated from this data. Reports include:
daily, weekly, monthly and year to date as well as an in-
active and individual account reports. Output may be on the
teletype or an optional lineprinter.

The DECtape for this system is available directly From the
author, to insure the most current release.

Minimum Hardware:
TSS/8 - 22B, DECtape
Storage Requirement:
2K
Source Language:
PAL-D

Catalog: July 1974
