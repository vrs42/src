I-O TAGS
L-35b
Author: Dr. C. M. Malpus, University of Leeds, Leeds, England
The I-O TAGS program contains defintions of the tags used in the DF.INOUT program (DECUS No. L-35A) including most of the functional ones of DBLFLT. It should be added to user's manuscripts when these are to be converted separately to DF.INOUT or when DF.INOUT and DBLFLT are called in binary form from LINCtape in the program.
Source Language: LAP6-3L
Catalog: November 1969
