TEXT TTY Subroutines
L-28
Author: Dr. W. H. Calvin, University of Washington, Seattle, Washington
This subroutine types out a list of LINC characters generated by LAP6 quotation mark method, analogous to the way in which Q & A Subroutine displays them. Control is restored at the location following the end of the list, marked by a 77 half-word.
Catalog: November 1969
