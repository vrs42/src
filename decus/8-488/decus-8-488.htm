Geoffrey Chase, Portsmouth Abbey School, Portsmouth,
Rhode Island

This program reads a binary tape on the low speed reader and
punches out a relocated copy on the low speed punch. Address
instructions are augmented by an integer number of
pages selected by the user .

Warning: It will not augment address pointers; main use will be
with routines one page or less in length.

Minimum Hardware:
4K PDP-8; (HSR/P optional)
Restrictions:
Field pseudo-ops will not be copied;
field instructions will
Source Language:
PAL III
