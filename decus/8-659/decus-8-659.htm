This is an OS/8-handler for a fast VT05. This handler will
supply the necessary fillers after a CR/LF. It will also allow
you to paginate the file on the display; i.e. , it will wait
after each page (form feed or 20 lines) to allow you to read
the display.

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8/E (only), VT05 at 600
baud or above
Other Programs Needed:
OS/8 Operating System
Storage Requirement:
l page
Source Language:
PAL-8
