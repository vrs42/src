Roulette
8-331

Ronald Servi and Leslie Servi, Lexington High School,
Lexington, Massachusetts

This program plays a game of roulette. The user has 100
chips to start, with which he can bet on any number from 0 to
20. Three bets are allowed for every spin of the wheel on 1,
2 or 4 numbers.

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8
Other Programs Needed:
CINET-BASIC (DECUS NO.
8-159)
Source Language:
CINET-BASIC
