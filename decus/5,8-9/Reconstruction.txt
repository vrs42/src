The image I found on bitsavers is decus-5-9.bad.
It has a problem where one of the data words on 
the 0600 page is missing either it's high or low 
half.

The file decus-5-9.err "fixes" this by adding a 
"040" before "007", forming a call to the floating 
point package, and aligning the data thereafter 
to look like instructions.  The result still has 
a sizeable checksum error, so there must still 
be additional errors.

The file foo.{pal,lst,bin} is a disassembly of 
decus-5-9.err, including any errors.  It does
point out that most of the content after 1200
appears to be text messages.
