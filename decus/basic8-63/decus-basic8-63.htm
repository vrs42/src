MAMII and MAMID
BASIC8-63
Submitted by: F. G. McIntosh
Abstract: "MAMII"-input version, "MAMID"-data version. The programs provide the functions of addition, multiplication and inversion using either 'input' statements or 'read' and 'data' statements. Both programs allow retention of solutions so that 'chain-type' calculations may be performed. Real matrices only.
