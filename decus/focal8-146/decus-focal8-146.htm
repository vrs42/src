Zeller's Congruence/Day of the Week
FOCAL8-146

Thomas J. Ford, White Mountains Regional High School,
Whitefield, New Hampshire

A demonstration program in which Zeller's Congruence is
applied to calculate the day of the week following input of
month, day and year. Input is self-terminating.
