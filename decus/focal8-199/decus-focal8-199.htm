Stock Market Game
FOCAL8-199

Ronald Papa, Hamden High School, Hamden, Connecticut

This game simulates buying and selling of stocks based on the
exchange's most basic principles. The operator has a choice
of three different stocks to deal with. Each is preset and rises
and falls randomly within a range of ± 3.5. Starting with
$10,000 the player continues until all his money and stocks
are lost or until he chooses to stop with whatever 'profit' or
'loss' he has taken.

Restrictions:
