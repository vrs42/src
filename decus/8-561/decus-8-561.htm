Revised HELP Loader for High Speed Reader and New BIN Loader
8-561

Frank Palmisano, 7 Brentwood Road, Hazlet, New Jersey

Through the use of auto-indexing, interrupt, and a minimum
of other commands, the HELP loader has been shortened to
seven steps, making it quicker to initialize the system.

The BIN loader has been reduced from 1438 locations to 1008
locations, allowing room for the TC01 bootstrap and leaving
the data break locations open.

Minimum Hardware:
4K PDP-8, HSR
Other Programs Needed:
RIM Loader
Restrictions:
Must be loaded in field zero.
Uses Interrupt
Source Language:
PAL

Catalog:July 1973
