Improved Multiply Loop for FOCAL FOCAL
focal8-280
Author: Jim Van Zee
University of Washington, Seattle, WA
Source Language: PAL-8
Other Software Required: FOCAL '69 (DEC-D8-AJAE)

Abstract: This 34 word patch provides a 25-35% reduction in FOCAL's
multiply time with a PDP-8/E, F, or M computer. Use is made of
the MQ register. The patch is 8 words shorter than the original code and
works with FOCAL '69 or FOCAL '71 and presumably most other
versions as well.
