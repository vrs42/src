FIDDLEX
L-56
Author: I. D. Pugsley, Department of Physiology and Biophysics, University of Washington, Seattle, Washington
The program provides a convenient means of editing a LAP6 index. It is capable of inserting, deleting, modifying or rearranging entries.
Source Language: LAP6
Storage Requirement: The manuscript for FIDDLEX occupies 42(8) blocks on tape; the binary occupies 5 blocks. During execution the program occupies 8 memory quarters.
Catalog: November 1969
