Teletype Conversion Routines
12-8
Author: Donald A. Overton, Ph.D., Eastern Pennsylvania Psychiatric Institute, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania
These routines provide various types of conversion from ASCII teletype code to binary, and vice versa. By using the appropriate routine, 1-8 octal or decimal digits may be converted from ASCII to single or double precision binary, and conversely. Some routines can search and decode QANDA answer fields. Most routines are by D. J. Nichols and have previously been circulated for use with LINC keyboard codes (DECUS NO. L-46 to L-50).
Source Language: LAP6-DIAL
Catalog: July 1974
