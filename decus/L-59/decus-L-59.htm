INDEX L4
L-59
Author: Paul F. Sullivan, National Aeronautics and Space Administration, Cambridge, Massachusetts
INDEX L4 resides on a LAP6-3L tape on unit 0 and examines the index tables of the various manuscript files of a LAP4 tape on unit 1. It is designed for use with the program TRANSLT6 in converting LAP4 manuscripts into LAP6-3L manuscripts and requires the program DISPATCH to control interactions between LAP6-3L, INDEX L4, and TRANSLT6.
Minimum Hardware: LINC-8
Other Programs Needed: TRANSLT6, DISPATCH (DEC-L8-MAA0-UA)
Source Language: LAP6-3L
Catalog: November 1969
