LOAD31K, A Loader for DIAL-MS and 32K of Core
12-152
Author: John R. Raines, Northwestern University Medical School, Chicago, Illinois
The DIAL system offers only an 8K binary work area and hence no facilities for loading programs into memory fields above Field 1. This program loads up to 4 binaries and all but the last 1400(8) words of core.
Minimum Hardware: DIAL-MS Configuration, 32K of Core, EAE
Other Programs Needed: DIAL-MS; DUAL32; CREF32 (These. files are all included on tape)
Source Language: DUAL (DECUS NO. 12-120a)
Catalog: July 1974
