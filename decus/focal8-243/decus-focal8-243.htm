Analysis of Variance for One-Two-and Three-Treatment
FOCAL8-243
Designs for a PDP-8

Robert Breaux, Texas Tech University, Lubbock, Texas

These programs provide a quick and easy analysis of variance.
Modification of error terms to fit particular needs in biology,
agriculture, medicine, etc., can be done easily. Output
includes terms for pooling error terms, mean comparisons and
trend analysis.
