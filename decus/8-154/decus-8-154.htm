SWAP
8-154

David M. Kristol, Moore School of Electrical Engineering,
University of Pennsylvania, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania

Using self-contained I/O, SWAP may be employed to load
the disk from or dump the disk onto DECtape. It is faster
and more verstile than the Disk to DECtape FAILSAFE.

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8 with DF-32 and TC01
DECtape

Source Language:
PDPMAP (DECUS No. 8-166)

Storage Requirement:
6008 + 42008 buffer

Catalog: November 1969
