Multiple Equation Graphing on a Teletype
FOCAL8-97

R. Bradford Malt, Wellesley High School, Wellesley,
Massachusetts

This program graphs up to 9 equations simultaneously on an
ASR33 or similar teletype. It requires only one type head
pass per line, providing considerable speed. Provisions are
made for error condition checks, and correction of specifica-
tion overflow is automatic.

Minimum Hardware:
PDP-8, ASR33
Source Language:
FOCAL

Catalog:July 1973
