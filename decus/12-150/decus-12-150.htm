XPIP10: PDP-10 DECtape to LINCtape Converter
12-150
Author: Douglas E. Wrege, Georgia Institute of Technology, Atlanta, Georgia
PIP10 is a PIP for PS/8 that handles PDP-10 DECtapes This version reads PDP-10 DECtapes directly via the TC12F hardware option on PDP-12 LINCtape drives.
Minimum Hardware: 8K PDP-12, TC12-F option
Other Programs Needed: PS/8, OS/8 or DECsystem 8 (DECUS NO. 8-646)
Source Language: PAL
Catalog: July 1974
