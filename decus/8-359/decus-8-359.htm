Hi-Q Game Playing Program
8-359

M. L. Fichtenbaum and R. E. Peterson, General Radio
Company, Concord, Massachusetts

Hi-Q is a game played with pegs on a board. This program
plays Hi-Q and finds solutions by means of a tree of possible
move patterns. Several different printouts are available,
selected by the switch register.

Minimum Hardware:
4K PDP-8 and Teletype
Source Language:
GR's PDP-8/1130 Assembly,
similar to PAL III

Catalog:July 1973
