Talking Eights
8-537

Louise Gerhart, Digital Equipment Corporation, Maynard,
Massachusetts

This program transmits and receives synchronous messages
between two PDP-8/Es. Messages are entered via teletype so
that operators at remote points may hold a "conversation."

Minimum Hardware:
2 PDP-8/Es equipped with 4K
core, DP8E Synchronous
Interface Modems and Lines
Storage Requirement:
4K
Source Language:
PAL-8

Catalog: July 1973
