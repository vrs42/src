Verify Paper Tape (12K)
8-471

J. Shanahan
Submitted by: J. H. McClure, E. I. duPont de Nemours and
Co., Wilmington, Delaware

This is a program to verify that a tape is the same as the one
from which it was copied, or that two copies of a tape are
the same. It is applicable to a PDP-8 system having at least
12K core.

Minimum Hardware:
12K PDP-8 with EAE

Catalog:July 1973
