Advanced Averager Improvement
8-536

Dr. J. L. Blom, Laboratory Ergonomic Psychology, TNO,
Amsterdam, The Netherlands

This is a series of listings for five overlays to use the Advanced
Averager program under the PS/8 monitor system on the LAB-
8/I. Added is a sixth part (on paper tape) which writes the
calculated data to any of the output devices under the PS/8
system.

Minimum Hardware:
LAB-8/I with 8K core; 2
DECtapes
Other Programs Needed:
PS/8 Monitor System, Advanced
Averager (DEC-LB-U17C-PB)
Restrictions:
Runs only on 8K
Source Language:
PAL-D

Catalog:July 1973
