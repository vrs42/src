4K DISK/LINCTAPE MONITOR
12-188
Author: Mark J. Hyde, 209 Ardsley Drive, DeWitt, New York
The 4K Linctape monitor is the disk/DECtape monitor with patches to allow it to use TC12 Linctape. The documentation contains many patches which are also of use to PDP-8 users of the disk/DECtape.
Minimum Hardware: PDP-12, TTY, any disk or LINCtape
Storage Requirement: 4K
Source Language: PAL-D
Catalog: May 1975
