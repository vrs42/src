NIM
FOCAL8-107

Kenneth McCord, Highland Park High School, Highland
Park, Illinois

The game of NIM consists of three columns of coins where the
number of coins in each column is different. The object of
the game is, by alternating turns with the computer, to re-
move all the coins from the playing board. The one who
removes the last coin or coins is declared the winner.

Minimum Hardware:
4K PDP-8
Other Programs Needed:
FOCAL with extended functions
deleted. Also delete "=" sign
Source Language:
FOCAL-69

Catalog: July 1973
