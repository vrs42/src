Chromaticity Diagram
8-578

R. Jacot, Integra A. G., Wallisellen, Switzerland

From 40 measured data of the spectral transmittance of a
material, the program computes and prints the coordinates in
the chromaticity diagram and the transmittance for the colour
temperatures 2854°K (C.I.E. standard source A) and for
2360°K.

Minimum Hardware:
4K PDP-8, TTY
Other Programs Needed:
Floating Point Package 1
(DEC-08-YQl B-PB) 4/17/70
Source Language:
PAL III

Catalog: July 1973
