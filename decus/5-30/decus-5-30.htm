GENPLOT: General Plotting Subroutines for the PDP-5
5-30

M. Adamowicz, Department of Electrical Engineering,
New York University, New York City, New York

This self-contained subroutine is for the PDP-5 with a 4K
memory and a CalComp incremental plotter. The subroutine
can move (with the pen in the up position) to locations (x, y),
make an "x" at this location, draw a line from this present
position to location (x, y) and initialize the plotter location
counters.

Catalog: November 1969
