STAT, Version: August 1976
8-660

Author: Lars Palmer, AB Hassle, Sweden

Operating System: OS/8 Source Language: FORTRAN IV, RALF
Memory Required: 12-16K Keywords: Statistics

Abstract: STAT is a large FORTRAN IV package consisting of one large
program for the statistical analyses and several accessory programs
specifically for manipulation on the data matrix and for analyses of a more
complex nature that cannot be covered by the main program.

Restrictions: STAT has been tested for many years on the PDP-8 and has
run satisfactorily. However, we do not have a PDP-8 on site to perform
any type of testing to the program. Therefor, it is released to DECUS on a
strict "AS IS" basis. No support will be available for this program.

Note: Full utilization (BATCH and all files) requires 16K memory.
