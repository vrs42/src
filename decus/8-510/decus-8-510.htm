P8COR - Overlay for 8K PAL-D Assembler for 4K Disk
Monitor System (DECUS NO. 8-333)
8-510

Arthur L. Pike, Tufts University, Medford, Massachusetts

P8COR is an overlay that permits most of the features of
DECUS NO. 8-333 to work as a stand-alone program for 8K
users when disk or DECtape are not availablie.

Minimum Hardware:
8K PDP-8/I with ASR33 (Can
use HSR/P if available)
Other Programs Needed:
DECUS NO. 8-333
Source Language:
PAL III (Assembled with
DECUS NO. 8-333)

Catalog:July 1973
