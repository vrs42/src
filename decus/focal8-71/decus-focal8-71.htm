FOCAL Golf Program for the PDP-8 (8K) Computer
FOCAL8-71

Gilbert S. Fair, Digital Equipment Corporation, Northbrook,
Illinois

This program simulates the playing of golf, including the shot
selection options of club, power and direction together with
numerous variations of these selections, to more closely re
semble the actual experience of a golfer "on the links."

Minimum Hardware:
8K PDP-8, ASR33
Storage Requirement:
7000-8000 words (including
FOCAL)
Miscellaneous:
Takes about one hour for 9 holes
(dependent on user reaction)
Source Language:
FOCAL - 69

Catalog:July 1973
