PLOT3D, Pseudo 3-Dimensional Perspective Display for the PDP-12
12-115
Author: Michael F. Lubozynski and John A. Freeman, Vanderbilt University School of Medicine, Nashville, Tennessee
PLOT3D is a LINC-mode pragram which will produce a pseudo 3-dimensional display of up to 16 256-word waveforms, shown at any desired perspective (and scale) and interconnected by a user-specified number of contour lines.
Minimum Hardware: 8K PDP-12
Restrictions: All input must be in octal
Source Language: DIAL-MS
Catalog: July 1974
