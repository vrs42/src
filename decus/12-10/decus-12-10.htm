FOCAL Library (LINCtape FOCAL for the PDP-12)
12-10
Author: Roger A. Nace, University of Washington, Anesthesia Research Center, Seattle, Washington
This is a 4K FOCAL library system to call FOCAL programs from LINCtape., Up to 62(10) programs may be stored on one tape. An index routine may be called which displays the index of the library. Through this program the index may be updated and new programs added to the system.
Source Language: LAP6-DIAL
Catalog: July 1974
