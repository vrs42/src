Wilcoxon-White Two Sample Rank Test
8-350

Jens G. Rosenkrantz, M. D., Childrens Hospital of Los
Angeles, Los Angeles, California

This nonparametric statistical test may be used in comparing
unpaired samples and assigns ranks to the pooled measurements,
comparing the ranks as ordinal numbers in two groups.

and punch
