Alpha-Numeric Display Program
8-514

Ralph Norman Haber, University of Rochester, Rochester,
New York

A program to display characters, along with a driver program
written in FORTRAN, and a subroutine program for listening
for button presses and recording reaction time in psychological
experiments.

Minimum Hardware:
4K PDP-8, 34D Display or
eguivalent
Source Language:
PAL and FORTRAN

Catalog: July 1973
